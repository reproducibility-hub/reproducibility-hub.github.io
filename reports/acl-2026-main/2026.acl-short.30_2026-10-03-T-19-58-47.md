### Summary of the comparison
- **Table 1 (Pythia blind), 4/4 rows match** (AUC and TPR1%): BoW 0.506 vs 0.505, TF-IDF 0.502 vs 0.503, W2V 0.490 vs 0.490, BERT 0.497 vs 0.497. For BERT this needs the release's default batch size 1; at batch size 8 the padded BERT [CLS] features change and best-of-3 becomes 0.515.
- **Table 2 (Pythia), LOSS / Min-K / Min-K++ / Zlib / ReCaLL: 25 rows × 2 metrics, all within tolerance.** Most AUCs and TPRs are identical to 3 decimals.
- **Table 2 MoPe.** With the released score: 70m 0.513 / 0.015, 160m 0.505 / 0.017, 410m 0.496 / 0.008, 1b 0.505 / 0.015, 2.8b 0.510 / 0.012. Paper: 0.487/0.025, 0.495/0.015, 0.504/0.014, 0.495/0.015, 0.493/0.006.
  - Negating the released score reproduces 70m–1b **exactly** (0.4869/0.025, 0.4946/0.015, 0.5039/0.014, 0.4952/0.015).
  - For 2.8b the negated AUC is within SE (0.490 vs 0.493) but TPR1% is not (0.020 vs 0.006). This is probably because the authors could not have run the released 2.8b MoPe on an 80 GB A100 (see notes).
  - Either way MoPe is never significantly above 0.5. MoPe on 70m with 5 more seeds gives 0.509–0.513 (released sign), and 160m gives 0.503–0.507.
  - The paper's remark that "MoPe … 2.5x above the random baseline as measured by TPR1% on the 70m model" only holds for the sign-flipped score. With the released score it is 1.5x.
- **Table 3 (OLMo blind), trend match.** Best-of-3 AUCs are 0.501 / 0.501 / 0.501 / 0.521 vs the paper's 0.491 / 0.491 / 0.494 / 0.496. BERT-NN 0.521 is 1.7 SE above 0.5 (best of 3, not significant).
- **Table 4 (OLMo MIAs), trend match.** LOSS 0.495 (paper 0.505), Min-K 0.495 (0.504), Min-K++ 0.497 (0.490), Zlib 0.497 (0.499), ReCaLL 0.498 (0.521). Every AUC is within ~1 SE of 0.5, and so are 4/5 of the paper's; the paper's ReCaLL 0.521 is itself only 1.6 SE above 0.5. The OLMo eval subset is not documented, and no window of the released OLMo dataset reproduces the paper's model-free Zlib row (0.499/0.019), so exact agreement isn't expected.
- **Extra (not in the paper).** A correct Carlini zlib attack (loss / zlib bytes) on the same points gives AUC 0.486–0.502 for all models (`logs/carlini_zlib_ratio.txt`). The released `LossZLIB` gives 0.5008 for every Pythia size, i.e. it reduces to the model-free zlib length.

## Why it isn't a clean REPRODUCED
1. The release doesn't run: a core module that the entry point imports is missing and had to be rebuilt.
2. The paper's MoPe numbers correspond to the opposite sign of the released MoPe score.
3. The OLMo appendix numbers match only in trend, because the evaluation subset is undocumented.

The paper's headline conclusion holds in every run: blind baselines and published MIAs are all at AUC ≈ 0.5 on the checkpoint-based splits.
