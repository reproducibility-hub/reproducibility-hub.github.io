## Codebase completeness: MISSING_CORE
Changes made to `./codebase` (uncommitted; `git diff` there):
1. `src/generation_mixin.py` — **added `_beam_search`** (about 140 lines). `generate()` dispatches to `self._beam_search(..., process_reward_model=prm)` for `generation_mode="beam_search"`, but no such method exists in the release. The inherited HF method neither accepts a PRM nor handles latents, so the release's `run_tts_with_rm.sh` beam-search runs cannot work. Our version, written from the paper's Appendix D:
   - B beams, K MC-dropout candidates per beam per latent step, each scored by the LatentRM at its newest latent;
   - cumulative reward, top-B pruning;
   - the best beam is decoded greedily with dropout off, as in `_sample`.
2. `src/infer_gpt2_rm.py`:

   - (c) added an optional `--prm_fix_position_ids` flag (default False = released behaviour);
   - (d) BoN mode additionally prints `Accuracy_posfix`, the same samples re-scored with padding-aware position ids. The released selection is unchanged;
   - (e) added an optional `--dropout_p` (default None = released behaviour).
3. `src/dataset.py` — **fixed a dedent bug in `DataCollatorForLatentRM`.** The label-assignment block sat outside the for-loop, so only the last sample of each batch got labels, and *no* sample got labels when `corrects` is absent. That breaks BCE training and makes the per-epoch validation metrics (which use this collator) use only one sample per batch.
4. `src/annotate_data.py` — defined `self.num_latents = latent_length`. It was referenced but never set, so annotation crashed immediately.
5. `src/models/coconut.py` — efficiency only: last-position-only logits (`logits_to_keep`), because full-vocab logits at prefill OOM for N=64. Verified bit-identical (deterministic 34.1168 before/after).

## Discrepancies
**Code vs paper**
- [major] **PRM scoring at inference** (`infer_gpt2_rm.py`, BoN). The PRM gets left-padded sequences without position ids, so samples with shorter answers get shifted positions. Training uses right padding, so positions are correct there. With the fix, BoN@8 on GSM-Test goes from 33.1 to 35.8 (MV is 34.0).
- [major] **Beam search missing.** It is described in the paper (Sec. 5.3, App. D, Fig. 6), but `_beam_search` is not implemented.
- [major] **BCE collator dedent bug** (`dataset.py`): the BCE ablation gets no training labels.
- [major] **Answer decoding with dropout.** For CoLaR (`infer_llama.py`: `explicit_do_sample=do_sample`) and in the annotation rollouts, dropout stays on while decoding the answer tokens. Algorithm 1 decodes the answer with the deterministic fθ.
- [minor] **Aggregation dropout rate.** Sec. 5.3 says MC-dropout p=0.2. `infer_gpt2_rm.py` never sets p, so it uses the checkpoint default 0.1, on residual, attention-probability and embedding dropout. The paper says "after the feed-forward layer"; `infer_gpt2.py` uses residual dropout (after the attention and MLP projections).
- [minor] **LatentRM training hyperparameters.** Paper: lr 1e-5, 10 epochs. `train_coconut.yaml`: lr 1e-4, 30 epochs with early stopping, `paged_adamw_8bit`.
- [minor] **Rollouts per step.** Paper M=128 vs `run_annotation.sh` `--n_samples_per_step=64`.
- [minor] **Dataset sizes.** Released MultiArith has 580 questions (paper: 600) and GSM-Hard has 1,318 (paper: 1,319).

**Paper-internal**
- Table 2: the text says Best-of-N with N=8; the caption says N=32.
- Table 2 MV on GSM-Hard is 6.1, but Figure 6 shows MV@8 ≈ 7.3 (and MV@32 ≈ 7.4).
- Figure 2: COCONUT GSM-Test MC-dropout coverage@1 ≈ 36.5 (above the deterministic 34.0). Figure 6 gives 31.3 for a single MC-dropout sample of the same model on the same data.
- Sec. 6.1 recommends σ∈[0.5, 0.7] for GPT-2-style models, but Figure 2 uses σ=1.4 for CODI.
