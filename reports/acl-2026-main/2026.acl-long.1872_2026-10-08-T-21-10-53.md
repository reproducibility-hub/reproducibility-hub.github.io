## What the paper claims and what we ran

**Claims**
1. Under their respective best configurations, IPA tokenizers beat Text tokenizers on 10 intrinsic metrics. The largest gains are in compression and cross-lingual equity (Fig. 3/5). The gains concentrate on non-Latin and unseen scripts (Fig. 4).
2. Across the 32 configuration pairs, IPA wins more often (158 vs 118 "trophies", Fig. 2).
3. GPT-2 models pretrained on IPA match the Text models downstream on XNLI and PAWS-X (Fig. 6), while compressing the inputs better (Fig. 7).

**What the release contains**
- The full pipeline code.
- Two HF checkpoints, `Mikki99/gpt2-ipa-opt` and `Mikki99/gpt2-text-opt`, each with its SentencePiece tokenizer.
- Not released: the Subopt tokenizers or models, the other 62 tokenizers, and the processed data.

**Data-access constraints.** CulturaX (training data) and FLORES+ (evaluation data) are HF datasets gated behind license terms. I did not accept license terms on the user's account. Substitutes:
- **FLORES-200** dev+devtest (public tarball from `dl.fbaipublicfiles.com`) instead of FLORES+. It has the same 2009 sentences per language. The exact Text Opt TFG match below suggests the substitution is harmless.
- **WikiPron** checked out at commit `967ceec` (Apr 2025). File counts match the paper's Table 4 exactly for 23 of 24 languages. The exception is ar: the paper lists 2,252 words, which matches no WikiPron version; we used `ara_arab_broad` with 13,339.
- **mC4** (`allenai/c4`, ungated) instead of CulturaX, for the tokenizer-grid experiment only. CulturaX is derived from mC4 and OSCAR. We sampled 100 MB instead of 1 GB, using the released per-strategy proportions.

### Experiments run

**A. Intrinsic evaluation of the released Opt tokenizers** (Fig. 3/4/5) — full scale, released scripts:
- G2P of the evaluation data: `convert_epitran.py`, `convert_phonemizer.py` (el, he), `clean_ipa.py`.
- Epitran IPA column for WikiPron: `wikipron_add_epitran.py`.
- Evaluation: `scripts/eval/run_intrinsic.py`.
- Win rate, mean z and d_z (Appendix N, Eqs. 24–27) computed with `scripts_ours/analyze_intrinsic.py`; the release has no code for these.

**B. Downstream fine-tuning of the released GPT-2 Opt checkpoints** (Fig. 6/7) — full scale:
- XNLI on 13 languages and PAWS-X on 7, in all three regimes (en-only, multilingual, monolingual), for both models.
- Released `scripts/gpt2/finetune/finetune.py` with the Table 10 hyperparameters: steps 6128 / 3860, learning rate 1e-4 / 3e-5, effective batch 256 (32 × 8 gradient accumulation on 1 GPU), cosine schedule, warmup 0.06, bf16, seed 42, early stopping with patience 3. Model selection used `--metric_for_best_model loss`, because the paper selects on validation loss.
- Caches were built with the released `build_xnli_split` / `build_pawsx_split`, through a parallel runner.
- Monolingual-en is the same run as en-only, since the training and validation data are identical.
- We also ran en-only with the code's default model selection (the README command) for all four model/task pairs ("as-released").
- ΔCR on the formatted test inputs (Fig. 7): `scripts_ours/bench_compression.py`, which reuses the released metric functions.

**C. Tokenizer grid on substitute data** (Fig. 2) — scaled down to 100 MB of mC4:
- Released steps: `stream_culturax_to_files` (pointed at `allenai/c4`), `filter_lang_leakage.py`, `sentence_split.py`, Epitran G2P, `clean_ipa.py`.
- Our CJK chunking step (see below).
- All 64 tokenizers trained with `train_grid.py` and evaluated with `run_intrinsic.py`.
- Fig. 2 trophy count computed by `scripts_ours/fig2_trophies.py`. The released ranking uses `rank_tokenizers.py`.

**D. Control experiment (not in the paper): keep combining marks.**
- Same pipeline as C, but the released `clean_line` regex is extended to keep `\p{M}`. This is a monkeypatch in `scripts_ours/sample_mc4_keepmarks.py`; the released source is unchanged.
- Run for the two Opt-related strategies (byte-uniform and data-smoothed) at 200k vocabulary.

**Not run**
- GPT-2 pre-training of the four models (≈7 GPU-h each). It needs gated CulturaX, and the Opt checkpoints are released.
- The Subopt GPT-2 models: neither the models nor their tokenizers are released.
- Table 7 (300k vocabulary).

## Why it is not fully reproduced
- **Gated data.** CulturaX and FLORES+ require accepting license terms; we used FLORES-200 and mC4 instead. Because of this, GPT-2 pre-training, the Subopt models and the exact 64-tokenizer grid could not be redone.
- **Missing G2P pieces.** The Epitran mapping fixes and the zh/ja chunking that Appendix D says are "accessible in our codebase" are not released. This causes the ur and tr win-rate flips and makes ur IPA lossy (21% of characters unmapped).
- **Figure-read values.** Most paper numbers exist only in figures, so the comparisons carry about ±0.02 reading error.

## Discrepancies

**Code vs. paper**
1. **Major — `culturax.clean_line` strips all Unicode combining marks.** The regex `[^\p{L}\p{N}\p{P}\p{Zs}]` deletes Devanagari matras and Thai/Lao vowel signs. The paper describes this step as removing only "non-linguistic artifacts". The released Text Opt tokenizer carries the artifact, which inflates IPA's advantage on hi/th/lo (control D).
2. **Major — Epitran mapping fixes (sr, ur, ar, fa, hi, fi, tr) and zh/ja chunking are absent.** The paper says they are in the codebase. Effect: the ur and tr win-rate flips, and lossy Urdu IPA.
3. **Major — default model selection keeps the worst checkpoint.** `finetune.py` defaults to `metric_for_best_model=accuracy` with `greater_is_better=False`, and the README command uses these defaults. Paper Table 10 selects by validation loss. Effect: XNLI en accuracy 0.649 instead of 0.740 for Text Opt.
4. **Minor — the released Text Opt tokenizer differs from the paper's description.** Its `trainer_spec` shows it was trained on the data-smoothed sample (`..._unb_smooth`) with `character_coverage=1.0`. Sec. 4.5 and the README say data-proportional; Table 8 says 0.9995.
5. **Minor — `plotting/fig2.py` is not Fig. 2.** It implements the per-mode ranking heatmap (Fig. 11 style), not the paired trophy count.

**Paper-internal**
6. Text Opt is "data-proportional" in Sec. 4.5 and Appendix G, but "data-smoothed" in the Fig. 11 caption.
7. Sec. 5.1.2 says fi has WR = 0.9, but Fig. 4a shows fi at 1.0.
