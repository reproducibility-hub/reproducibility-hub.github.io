## What the paper claims

HistLens decomposes concept representations with the Layer-29 OpenSAE on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. It tracks SAE feature activations over time for four concepts (individual / society / nation / world) in two OCR'd periodicals, *New Youth* (1915–1926) and *The Guide*. The corpora total 3,277 issues and 8.0M characters.

Quantitative results:
- **Table 1** (Concept–Corpus Atlas): for each concept and corpus, the de-lexicalization ratio r̄, normalized entropy H, the peak year, and the strongest turning year with its signed intensity I.
- **Table 2** (cross-layer robustness, layers 06/14/22/29): peak year, turn year, and Avg. 2-gram Jaccard.
- **Fig. 5:** cross-corpus Jaccard@30.
- **Table 3:** historian validation of implicit cases.
- The remaining results are figures and qualitative interpretation.

## Why it cannot be reproduced

1. **Corpora not released.** The repo has no data and no download link; the README only describes the expected `sentences/<corpus>/*.txt` and `metadata/<corpus>/documents.jsonl` layout. The paper says the corpora were "compiled and curated" and digitized via OCR by the authors, and promises only to "document provenance". Web searches found no public copy of this corpus or of an equivalent full OCR corpus.
2. **Expert-validated concept base sets not released.** Every Table 1 and Table 2 quantity depends on the expert-validated SAE base sets / orientations S_c and their labels (Table 6). This covers concept magnitude m_i, the salient set (q=0.95), μ, A, p, H, peak, turn, I and r̄ (App. B.1–B.5, B.8). None of S_c is in the repo.
3. **No code for the reported metrics.** The repo does not implement r̄, H, the peak/turn year, I, the salient-set construction, Jaccard@K, or the cross-layer 2-gram Jaccard (checked by grep and by reading every module).

   What it does implement:
   - target-token SAE activation extraction;
   - yearly mean centers and cosine/Euclidean distances;
   - top-N cumulative-drift bases;
   - peak-pair evidence sentences;
   - wordclouds;
   - non-target (anchor-absent) activation analysis;
   - plots.

   Even with the corpora, Tables 1 and 2 would have to be written from the appendix and would still need the unreleased S_c.

## Discrepancies (code vs paper)

1. **Number of drifting bases (minor).**
   - Paper (App. B.7): Top-K drifting set with K=30.
   - Code: `run.py` passes `--top_n 15`, and the default in `identify_drift.py` is 10.
2. **What drift is computed over (major).**
   - Paper (App. B.2/B.6/B.7): drifting bases and trajectories are computed on the salient set (top 5% of concept magnitude m_i over the expert base set S_c).
   - Code: yearly centers and cumulative drift use every sentence containing the literal target string, with no salience filtering and no S_c.
3. **Reported metrics (major).**
   - Paper: the framework computes the Table 1 and Table 2 metrics.
   - Code: none of these metrics are implemented.
