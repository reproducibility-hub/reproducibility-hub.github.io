### Summary

**The numbers reproduce.** After small library-port fixes, the released activation-patching code reproduces the
paper's Section-5 appendix numbers *bit-for-bit*:
- **OLMo-2-1B, 4 contrastive tasks (full grid, 100 samples):**
  - Table 15 (logit): 120/120 entries exact.
  - Table 5 (t-stats): 120/120 exact.
  - Table 9 (rank): 110/120 exact; the other 10 have the same tuples and are 0.01 lower.
  - Tables 17/18 (3-layer): 240/240 exact.
- **OLMo-2-1B, BigBench:** implicatures, object_counting and snarks exact in Tables 13/10/6. metaphor_boolean does not
  reproduce (cause unknown).
- **OLMo-2-7B** (100 samples, restricted to the tuples the paper lists): all 23 cells of Tables 14/16/7 and 12/11/8 are
  exact — 690/690 rank, logit and t values.
- **Setting needed:** rank values require `--max_rank 100`; the released default is 1000.

**The paper's conclusions only partly survive.**
- **Superadditivity (central claim).** The paper says f(Li∪Lj) ≥ f(Li)+f(Lj) "holds to a significant degree for all models
  and tasks". That rests on `ttest_1samp(<0/1 booleans>, popmean=50)`, which is "significant" for any data; we reproduce
  the paper's t-values exactly, so this is the test that was run.
  - With a valid one-sided binomial test on the same data, every top pair is significant in only 14/47 model×task
    cells, and fewer than half are in 12/47. Examples: 1B-SFT/DPO ANIM:CAN_FLY, base-1B object_counting, and 7B-DPO
    snarks / metaphor_boolean / implicatures.
  - The released patching protocol overwrites the query token itself: there is no filler token, unlike the paper's
    description. For single-token queries the 2-layer patch is therefore identical to the single-layer patch, and the
    inequality reduces to f(Li) ≤ 0.
  - On multi-token queries only, the adjective tasks keep significance in just 2–5 of 8–9 pairs.
- **Linear probe ("98%<" for all clusters):** as released, 96.1 / 97.6 / 97.2% on OLMo-1B base/SFT/DPO, with per-class
  accuracy down to 89–92%.
- **Path tracing (Fig. 4, scaled down):** reproduces for the adjective tasks, only partly for the animal tasks.
- **Not runnable:**
  - The head-activity analyses (Tables 2/3, top-k with k>1, Jaccard/Spearman/bootstrap) — that code is not in the release.
  - The IA metric — it needs a closed GPT-5-nano judge.

## Compute, wall-clock and notes

- **Wall-clock:** about 22.6 h of the 48 h budget (2026-10-05 16:51 → 2026-10-06 15:30 CDT), on 1× H200 141 GB, 14 CPU
  cores and 111 GB RAM.
- **Throughput:** the patching code is CPU/launch-bound (~0.15–0.2 s per nnsight trace; ~100 s per sample per 1B run
  when run alone). We ran 12 1B runs concurrently (~230 s/sample each) to finish the 24 full-grid 1B runs (contrastive
  + BigBench) in ~13 h.
- **7B full grid:** 528 tuples, ~1000 s/sample under load, i.e. the paper's 24 h/task. This does not fit in the budget
  (12 cells × 24 h); hence the tuple-subset runs.
- **GPU OOM:** one transient OOM occurred when four 7B BigBench jobs (~33 GB each) overlapped with two 1B jobs. The
  affected cells were re-run from scratch; no partial outputs were used.
- **Determinism:** with the released determinism settings (`CUBLAS_WORKSPACE_CONFIG`, `use_deterministic_algorithms`),
  our values match the paper to the last printed digit. We compared only these printed values; bit-level equality of
  the raw tensors was not checked.
- **Cleanup:** the conda env was removed at the end (`conda env remove -p env`). `codebase/` (with uncommitted edits),
  `analysis/` (scripts + JSON results), `logs/`, the patching outputs under `codebase/experiments/output/`, and the
  probe datasets under `codebase/hidden_state_datasets/` are kept.
