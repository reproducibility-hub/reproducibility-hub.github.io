Short summary:
* **Reproduced well:** Table 1 (ordinal data: 35/36 entries identical), Fig. 1 (RSA of number embeddings: identical to the
  authors' values), the per-layer probing accuracies of the released Fig. 3 notebooks (all 7 models re-run, ±0–2 pts
  vs. the released outputs except a few unstable early-layer probes), Fig. 5b (held-out layer, Llama 1B, natural contexts:
  96–100 % on intermediate layers), Fig. 7 (Llama 3.2 3B ×/÷ output probing curves), Table 2's multiplication accuracy
  (90.38 % vs 90.4 %), and the qualitative steering result of Fig. 9a (param-sin > sin at every patched layer; same peak
  layers), though with ~0.7× the paper's magnitudes.
* **Not reproduced / contradicted:**
  * **Fig. 2b "perfect agreement" of top-63 Fourier frequencies across all models** is an artifact of a caching bug in the
    released notebook (every model is silently compared with OLMo-2-1B's own frequencies). With the bug fixed the
    number-token IoU@63 is 0.56–0.94 (mean 0.75), not 1.0; random pieces drop from the paper's 0.73–0.88 to ≈0.13.
    The weaker qualitative claim (numbers share frequencies far more than random pieces) still holds.
  * **Fig. 3 / §3.2 text ("both sinusoidal probes reach over 80 % of accuracy in all but two cases")**: the released
    `sec3.2` notebooks (param-sin repo) give param-sin < 80 % in 17/109 (model, layer) cases and *both* sinusoidal probes
    < 80 % in 11/109 (mostly early layers of the three Llama models, where param-sin gets 0–23 %). Fig. 3b's Llama points
    were evidently produced with a different probe configuration that only exists in the older numllama repo
    (`notebooks/newprobe/model_activations_probing_natural_contexts`); re-running that notebook for Llama 1B reproduces the
    figure's values (25/72/86/100… vs 16/81/91/98…).
  * **Fig. 5a (cross-layer transfer in natural contexts, Llama 1B)**: the released notebook
    `sec3.3/model_probe_transfer/llama1b_natural.ipynb` does not learn (0 % validation accuracy on layers 0–6 after 50 k
    steps each), and its later cells crash; the released copy has no saved outputs beyond step 0.
  * Table 2: subtraction/addition statistics and the ×/− model accuracies reproduce, but the ×/÷ extraction
    statistics do not (ours: P(extracted | incorrect) 89.5 % / 10.0 % vs 26.3 % / 94.4 %); the paper's own Fig. 8
    agrees with our values, so Table 2 appears internally inconsistent. Addition accuracy is 94.6 % (paper 100 %).

## Overall judgement
* **result_match = MIXED**: the ordinal-data table, RSA, per-layer probing in middle layers, output tracking and the
  steering ranking reproduce; the headline "almost perfectly universal" Fourier-frequency result (Fig. 2b/2c) is a
  bug artifact, the released Fig. 3 code contradicts the text's "all but two" claim for Llama models, and the released
  Fig. 5a notebook does not work.

  numbers, Table 1 / Fig. 3 / Fig. 12 / RSA-baseline as above, Fig. 7 needs only device/path config changes, but the
  steering pipelines (Fig. 9) do not run (decoder-layer API / hidden_dim mismatch crashes).

## Discrepancies
See `status.json` → `discrepancies` (11 entries). Main ones:

   produced Fig. 3b's Llama points (that config is only in the numllama repo).
