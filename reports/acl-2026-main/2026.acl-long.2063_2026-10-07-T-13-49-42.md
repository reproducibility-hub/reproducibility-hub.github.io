## Discrepancies
- **code_vs_paper (major)**: `build_variants` is a placeholder, so no code-switching happens. The paper's Sec. 3.3 describes four distinct code-switched variants.
- **code_vs_paper (major)**: The teacher is `DummyTeacher`, which returns `[]`, and the API teacher raises `NotImplementedError`. The paper uses LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct with K=5 samples and triplet embeddings.
- **code_vs_paper (major)**: `map_back_triplets` is a substring placeholder. The paper uses an alignment function Ψ.
- **code_vs_paper (major)**: The released data does not match the data in Sec. 4.1 and Table 1. The Spanish file is a Twitter emotion corpus, and the English file is unlabeled product reviews. Also, the paper states that "the data and code used in this study are shared".
- **paper_internal**: Sec. 5 obs. 3 claims gains of about 2.5–3.0 F1 over TT-CSW and about 2.0 over LACA on Indic. Table 2 gives 5.35 and 3.79 over TT-CSW (non-Indic and Indic) and 5.88 over LACA on Indic. The abstract says 5.3 and 3.8.
- **paper_internal**: Sec. 5 obs. 5 gives ablation drops that do not match Table 2:
  - Consistency filter: the text says 2.5–3.0 (Indic) and 1.5–2.0 (non-Indic); Table 2 gives 4.68 and 3.55.
  - Contrastive distillation: the text says 1.5–2.0; Table 2 gives 2.99 and 2.29.
- **paper_internal**: The paper says it uses ASTE-Data-V1, which is English-only, and that it does not derive or project opinion terms. Yet Table 1 reports opinion-term counts and gold triplets for FR/ES/NL/RU, whose SemEval-2016 data has no opinion annotations. The source of these labels is not identified.
