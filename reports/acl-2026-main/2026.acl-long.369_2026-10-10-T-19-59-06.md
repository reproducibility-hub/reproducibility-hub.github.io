### Pipeline
Our scripts are in `scripts/`; the released code is in `codebase/` (one uncommitted edit).

**`scripts/vise_data.py` (data and prompts)**
- Loads the QA CSVs with exactly the conventions of `process_dataset()` in `inference_example.py`.
- Second-round and mimicry prompts come from **calling the released `get_answer()`**, with `get_llm_response` monkey-patched to return the prompt text. The prompts are therefore byte-identical to the release.
- The first-round prompt is the paper's Appendix-E "Baseline Question Generation Template". The release has none.
- Judging uses the released `judge_process` (exact single letter) after a lenient leading-letter parse (`"A) man"` → `A`). Qwen-7B results are identical with and without the parse.

**`scripts/vise_vllm.py` (benchmark runner)**
- Runs round 1, then the 7 types, with vLLM 0.31: greedy decoding, the model's generation-config repetition penalty, ≤16 new tokens.
- Qwen2.5-VL settings:
  - Same video preprocessing as the released `get_llm_response`: qwen-vl-utils 0.0.11, `fps=1`, `max_pixels=360*420`.
  - fp16 for 7B.
  - Temporal positions as in transformers 4.53.
- Validation against the released HF `get_llm_response`: 199/200 identical answers. A full HF re-run of Qwen-7B (`qwen7b_hf_nohook`) gives avg MSS 28.9 vs vLLM 29.9 and accuracy 69.93 vs 69.95.
- InternVL2.5 settings, all as in the released `Hooks/intervention_internVL.py`:
  - 10 frames at `get_index` segment midpoints;
  - one 448-px tile per frame;
  - a `Frame{i}: <image>` prompt prefix.
- LLaVA-OV settings: 10 uniformly sampled frames, as in `Hooks/intervention_llava.py`.
- The paper never states the frame count used for the benchmark.

**`scripts/vise_steer_hf.py` (steering on ViSE)**
- Runs the same ViSE protocol in HF transformers.
- Imports the **released** `sycophancy_intervention` hook from `codebase/Hooks/*.py`.
- Uses the PCA artifacts produced by the **released, unmodified** `PCA Layers/*.py` and `Hooks/*.py` scripts (outputs in `steer/outputs*`).
- The hook is active in both rounds, which is what Table 5 implies.
- With `--alpha 0`, it produces the un-steered controls.

**Aggregation**
- `scripts/aggregate.py`: MSS = N_{C→I}/N_C, where N_C counts first-round-correct answers.
- `scripts/build_comparisons.py`: builds `results/comparisons.json`.

- **[major] MSRVTT bug.** The two-turn prompts show no answer choices for MSRVTT, i.e. half the benchmark.
- **[major] First-round lookup.** The MSVD/MSRVTT lookup (`baseline_item[2] == question_id`) has no not-found guard. If it never matches, a single fixed row is used for every question. Emulating this reproduces the shape of the paper's LLaVA row; this could not be verified.
- **[major] Steering rule.** Paper Sec. 5.2: subtract α·v/‖v‖, where v is the mean activation difference. Code: remove the projection onto the top-10 PCA components of *mean-centred* paired differences (α=1). Appendix H.2.2 mentions PCA, but the main text does not.
- **[major] Qwen hook is a no-op in the authors' env.** `Hooks/intervention_qwen.py` returns tuple layer outputs unchanged. In the declared env (transformers 4.53.3), Qwen2.5-VL decoder layers return tuples, so the released Qwen intervention would silently do nothing there. It works in our 5.17 env.
- **[minor] α values.** The paper says α is model-specific and 0.25 for LLaVA. The release has a single default, α=1.0 with k=10, and no per-model configs.
- **[minor] Calibration set.** The paper says 100 NExT-QA videos distinct from ViSE. The code uses the first 100 rows of lmms-lab/NExTQA MC test: 96 videos, 7 of which are in ViSE.
- **[minor] Data size.** The released QA files contain 6,362 MCQs; the paper says 6,367.
