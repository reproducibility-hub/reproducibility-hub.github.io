# Reproduction report — Double: Breaking the Acceleration Limit via Double Retrieval Speculative Parallelism (ACL 2026, 2026.acl-long.879)

**Verdict: PARTIALLY_REPRODUCED** · result match: **TREND_MATCH** (as released: TREND_MATCH) · coverage: **SCALED_DOWN** (3 of 5 Table-1 benchmarks; AR baseline throughput measured on prompt subsets) · codebase completeness: **MISSING_SCRIPTS**

Code: https://github.com/Sylvan820/Double-Spec (commit 85c577e), cloned to `./codebase`.

## Summary
* Ran Table 1 (greedy, batch size 1) for **all 5 model pairs** on the 3 benchmarks the release covers: HumanEval (164), GSM8K (first 100, as coded) and MT-Bench (80 questions, 2 turns). Ran **DOUBLE** (`--eval_mode para_sd`), the **AR** baseline (`--eval_mode large`) and the release's **PEARL** implementation.
* **The paper's qualitative claims hold on our hardware.**
  * DOUBLE is lossless up to bf16 near-ties.
  * It is clearly faster than AR (1.8–5.3×).
  * Its MAT is higher than PEARL's in all 15 settings.
  * It is faster than PEARL in all 15 settings with the primary metric. On the raw total-tokens/time metric it is faster in 14/15; LLaMA-2 MT-Bench is a near tie, 2.11× vs 2.14×.
  * Its HumanEval speedup exceeds the measured draft/target speed ratio C for every pair ("breaks the speedup limit").
* **The absolute speedups differ from Table 1 beyond the 5% tolerance.**
  * Lower for the 70B pairs and Deepseek-HumanEval: the headline LLaMA3.3-70B HumanEval is **3.06× vs the paper's 5.33×**.
  * Higher for Qwen3 and Deepseek GSM8K: the headline Qwen3-32B HumanEval is 2.61× vs 2.84×.
  * Only 1 of the 15 DOUBLE speedups is within 5% (LLaMA-2 GSM8K, 2.87× vs 2.83×).
  * The main reason is hardware. On 4×H200 with HF eager decoding, the draft/target speed ratio C we measured is 1.3–2.8, versus 1.6–5.0 on A100 in the paper. Our 70B AR runs at 18 tok/s versus about 5.7 tok/s in the authors' own logs (`codebase/exp/H_AR_llama3.3-70b`). Our PEARL numbers show the same shift, and PEARL MAT matches the paper closely (e.g. 10.78 vs 10.69, 7.41 vs 7.24).
* Not reproducible from the release:
  * CNN/DM and Alpaca: no data or scripts.
  * The ShareGPT prior datastore (Table 3).
  * Baselines other than PEARL/AR: Lookahead, PLD, Token Recycling, Ouroboros, SpS, EAGLE-3. The claim "beats EAGLE-3" is therefore NOT_COMPARABLE.

## Setup
* Hardware: 4× NVIDIA H200 141 GB, one Slurm job; jobs run strictly one at a time on all 4 GPUs. The paper used A100 80 GB with 70B models on 2 GPUs.
* Env: conda python 3.10, torch 2.5.1+cu121, transformers 4.51.3, accelerate 1.6.0, fschat 0.2.36. There is no requirements.txt; versions follow `install.sh` plus Qwen3's requirements.
* Models: identical to the paper. Gated meta-llama repos were not accessible, so I used weight-identical ungated mirrors: `unsloth/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct`, `unsloth/Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct`, `NousResearch/Llama-2-{7b,70b}-chat-hf`. Meta's official Llama-2 chat template was added to the Nous tokenizer configs. Other models: `deepseek-ai/deepseek-coder-{1.3b,33b}-instruct`, `Qwen/Qwen3-{0.6B,14B,32B}`.
* Hyper-parameters: as in the release's `scripts/run_double*.sh`, which match the paper.
  * γ = ⌈C_paper⌉: Qwen3-14B 2, Qwen3-32B 3, Deepseek 4, LLaMA 5.
  * Retrieval depth d = `pld_num_pred_tokens` = 10, n-gram 3, `--max_tokens 1024 --temp 0`.
  * As in those scripts, no retrieval cache/prior is used.
* Commands: `run_queue2.sh` + `queue*.txt`. Each job is:
  `accelerate launch --num_processes {2 for para_sd, 1 for AR} run_wrap.py benchmark/<eval_*.py> --data_path <dir> --eval_mode {para_sd|large} --gamma γ -n 1 -e <name> --draft_model <d> --target_model <t> --max_tokens 1024 --temp 0`
  `run_wrap.py` only prints MAT at exit and, with `PEARL=1`, routes `para_sd` to the release's PEARL function. The first Qwen3-14B jobs ran without the wrapper through `run_queue.sh`; Qwen3-14B GSM8K was rerun under the wrapper to get its MAT.
  Per-pair scripts: Qwen → `eval_humaneval_qwen3.py`, LLaMA-3 → `eval_humaneval_llama3.py`, Deepseek/LLaMA-2 → `eval_humaneval.py`; `eval_gsm8k.py` and `eval_mt_bench.py` for all pairs.
* Metrics (`analyze.py`):
  * Speedup = DOUBLE tokens/s ÷ AR tokens/s.
  * HumanEval/GSM8K: total new tokens / total wall time, as the scripts print it. **"Ours" excludes the first task**, because the released scripts start rank 0's timer before rank 1 has finished loading the target model. Raw values are given too.
  * MT-Bench: the script's own metric, mean per-turn tokens/s with the first writing turn dropped.
  * MAT = the scripts' `num_acc_tokens` mean.
* Differences from the paper:
  * AR throughput measured on subsets: first 20 HumanEval tasks, first 20 GSM8K questions, every 8th MT-Bench question (10 questions, 20 turns). AR always decodes 1024 tokens and its tokens/s has std < 1%. DOUBLE and PEARL ran on the full sets.
  * GPUs: H200 instead of A100.
  * The target model is spread over 4 GPUs by the release's `device_map='balanced_low_0'`.

### Other checks
* **Losslessness.** HumanEval/GSM8K outputs are identical to AR for Qwen3 (80/80) and LLaMA-3.3 (39/40). GSM8K accuracy of DOUBLE vs AR on the same subset: Qwen3-14B 0.95/0.95, LLaMA-3.3 0.96 (full 100) vs 0.90 (first 20 AR).
  * Deepseek (33/40), LLaMA-2 (17/40) and MT-Bench show divergences.
  * I re-decoded the divergence points with the target in **fp32**:
    * LLaMA-2-70B, 8 HumanEval cases: `tie_check_l2.json`.
    * Deepseek-33B, MT-Bench: `tie_check.py`, CPU.
  * Every divergence is a near-tie: top-2 logit gaps of 0.012–0.74, e.g. 'function' 19.564 vs 'Python' 19.545.
  * fp32 sides with AR 4×, with DOUBLE 3×, with neither 1×. The differences are therefore bf16 numerics, since DOUBLE verifies many tokens per forward pass. This is consistent with the paper's D.2 remark; it is not a correctness bug.
* **Retrieval-cache prior.** This is the release's stand-in for the paper's ShareGPT prior: `--use_retrieval_cache --warmup_task_count 10`, as in the top-level `run_double.sh`, warmed on the first 10 HumanEval tasks.
  * Qwen3-14B HumanEval: 1.93× (MAT 11.96) vs 1.94× without.
  * Qwen3-32B: 2.51× (MAT 9.28) vs 2.53×.
  * There is no gain, whereas Table 3 reports 2.35→2.84× for Qwen3-32B with the ShareGPT prior. This is NOT_COMPARABLE: the paper's prior is not in the release.
* **Not run.**
  * CNN/DM, Alpaca: no data or scripts in the release.
  * Table 2 (T=1.0), Table 3 (ShareGPT prior), Fig. 5/6 ablations.
  * Baselines without code in the release: Lookahead, PLD, TR, Ouroboros, EAGLE-3. SpS (`--eval_mode sd`) exists but was skipped for time.
  * "Outperforms EAGLE-3" → NOT_COMPARABLE.

## Result match
* `result_match = TREND_MATCH`.
  * DOUBLE > PEARL > AR (as described above), MAT_DOUBLE > MAT_PEARL in all settings, and the speedup exceeds C for all pairs.
  * Absolute speedups are mostly outside tolerance, in both directions. Headline: LLaMA3.3-70B HumanEval 3.06× vs 5.33×; Qwen3-32B HumanEval 2.61× vs 2.84×.
  * The claimed relative gain over PEARL is reproduced in sign. Its size varies: paper +9–49% per cell; ours +7% (LLaMA-3.3 GSM8K) to +92% (Deepseek MT-Bench).
* `result_match_as_released = TREND_MATCH`. Using the raw script aggregates, DOUBLE beats PEARL in 14/15 settings; LLaMA-2 MT-Bench is a near tie (2.11× vs 2.14× on total tokens/time). The only source edit is the model-path dictionary.

## Discrepancies
1. **(code_vs_paper, major) Prior datastore.**
   * Paper: a hierarchical datastore pre-populated from generic ShareGPT data (K=10 rounds, 9.5 MB), kept disjoint from the test sets.
   * Code: no ShareGPT builder. `--warmup_task_count K` warms up on the first K **test** tasks. `on_task_end` merges every evaluated task's output into the persistent cache (online accumulation over the test set). The main run scripts don't enable the cache at all.
2. **(code_vs_paper, major) Wall-time measurement.** There is no barrier after model loading. The first task's time includes rank 1's target-model loading, deflating the raw speedups. Examples:
   * Deepseek HumanEval: 2.51× raw vs 3.69× without the first task.
   * LLaMA-2 GSM8K: 1.75× vs 2.87×.
3. (code_vs_paper, minor) README vs repo: `build_datastore.py`, `double_retrieval` and `--datastore_path` don't exist.
4. (code_vs_paper, minor) Benchmark data:
   * `data/humaneval.jsonl` has 20 of 164 tasks.
   * GSM8K uses the first 100 questions with an 8-shot raw-text prompt, whereas the paper says it follows EAGLE-3.
5. (code_vs_paper, minor) The AR baseline ignores EOS and always decodes 1024 tokens, while DOUBLE/PEARL stop at EOS. The comparison is in tokens/s.
6. (code_vs_paper, minor) Device placement: the target uses `balanced_low_0` over all GPUs, vs "70B on two devices" on A100 in the paper.
7. (code_vs_paper, minor) MT-Bench `model_id` substring detection fails for the authors' own hard-coded Qwen/LLaMA-3 paths (NotImplementedError).
8. (paper_internal) Appendix C.1 says "three key metrics" but lists two.
