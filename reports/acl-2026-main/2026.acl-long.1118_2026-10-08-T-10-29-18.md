**Short version.** The release contains the full LAMCL training and evaluation code, plus a script for building the dataset (`data/makedata.py`). It does **not** include the paper's LLM-revised XSum datasets. Rebuilding them needs Qwen3-32B and Llama-3-8B-Instruct (both local) and DeepSeek-V3.2 and GPT-4o (API keys, not available here). We rebuilt the **Qwen3 and Llama3** datasets for all three tasks with the release's own prompts and trained the **unmodified** `LAMCL.py` on them.

On this rebuilt data the paper's LAMCL numbers come out close:
- **Rewrite and polish:** all four LLM/task cells within 1 AUROC point (e.g. Qwen3 rewrite 0.9436 vs 0.9451; Llama3 rewrite 0.9854 vs 0.9835).
- **Generate:** higher than the paper (0.994 vs 0.946 / 0.971).
- **Ablations:** the EBD module's gain reproduces on Qwen3. The LW module's and the CM module's contributions are not distinguishable from seed noise.

The data are rebuilt (LLM sampling at T=0.7, a different serving stack), so by the task rules every comparison is `NOT_COMPARABLE` and the verdict is `FAILED`. Apart from the missing data, we found no problem with the release.

## What the paper claims (targets)
- Table 1 (rewrite), Table 13 (polish), Table 14 (generate): LAMCL AUROC on XSum revised by Qwen3-32B, Llama-3-8B-Instruct, DeepSeek-V3.2 and GPT-4o (5000 XSum samples, 80/10/10 split). LAMCL beats SIMLLM, ImBD and others.
- Table 4 / Table 10: ablations of the LW module, the EBD module, and the CM connection.
- Fig. 5: short-text (<30 words) AUROC — Ours 0.9246 vs MCL 0.9084 (Qwen3 rewrite); 0.9262 vs 0.8950 (Qwen3 polish).
- Table 3/11 (cross-prompt/cross-LLM) and Table 5/15 (heterogeneous ImBD data): not attempted (see below).

## What we did (exact commands)
Environment: `conda create -p ./env python=3.11`. Then `pip install vllm==0.10.0 transformers==4.55.0 easydict==1.9 numpy==1.26.4 pandas==2.3.1 scikit-learn==1.7.0 tqdm==4.67.1`, which gives torch 2.7.1+cu126 and openai 1.90.0. One NVIDIA H200.

1. **Source data.** The README points to the SimLLM repo. `ext/SimLLM/dataset.csv` has 5000 XSum human sentences (column `human`), copied to `gen_data/xsum_source.csv`. This is the "5000 samples following Nguyen-Son et al. (2024)".
2. **Data construction** for local models, with the release's prompts:
   `python scripts/gen_vllm.py --model {llama3-8b|qwen3-32b} --input_csv gen_data/xsum_source.csv --out_dir gen_data`
   - The script imports `PROMPT_TEMPLATES`/`build_messages` from `codebase/data/makedata.py`, using the same system/user messages and chat template (`enable_thinking=False` for Qwen3).
   - Sampling: temperature 0.7, max 1024 new tokens, other sampling defaults from each model's `generation_config` (what HF `generate` uses).
   - Output schema is the same: `human, rewrite1=LLM(human), rewrite2=LLM(rewrite1)`, for 3 tasks × 2 LLMs = 6 CSVs of 5000 rows.
   - vLLM was used instead of per-sample HF `generate` for speed only.
3. **Training/eval** with the unmodified `codebase/LAMCL.py`, `dataset_MRT.py`, `loss.py`, `LW_module.py` and the paper hyper-parameters, which are the `train_config.py` defaults:
   - batch 16, lr 2e-5, 10 epochs, τ 0.05, m 0.999, queue 512, k 16, λ 0.1, p 0.1, 8 heads, RoBERTa-base.
   - Command: `python -u scripts/run_lamcl.py --data_file gen_data/<model>_<task>_dataset.csv --seed {0,1,2} --save_path runs/main_s<seed>`
   - `run_lamcl.py` only sets `data_file`, `SEED` and `save_path` (`data_file=[""]` in the shipped config) and calls `LAMCL.cl_train`.
   - Test AUROC is reported at the best-validation epoch, as the code does. The split is 80/10/10 with seed 42, giving 1000 test samples (500 human + 500 revised).
   - The paper reports one run (SEED=0). We also ran seeds 1 and 2.
4. **Ablations (Table 4 and Table 10)** — the release has no switches for them. We used a patched **copy**, `scripts/ablation_code/LAMCL.py`, run via `LAMCL_CODE=scripts/ablation_code ABL_NO_LW={0,1} ABL_EBD={cm,none,direct}`:
   - `ABL_NO_LW=1` removes π(L) from the hard-negative weighting.
   - `ABL_EBD=none` classifies from the plain-text [CLS].
   - `ABL_EBD=direct` classifies from the [CLS] of the concatenated text, without the CM module.
   - Seed 0, on Qwen3/Llama3 × rewrite/polish.
5. **Fig. 5 length analysis:** `python scripts/eval_len.py <run_dir>`. It splits test samples at <30 / ≥30 words and computes AUROC on each part. It re-scores the saved best checkpoint, and the overall AUROC equals the logged value for every run.
6. Aggregation: `scripts/aggregate.py` → `results_rows.json`, `results_len.jsonl`; `scripts/write_status.py` → `status.json`.

Job orchestration: `scripts/jobs.py` and `scripts/queue.sh`, 3 runs in parallel on the GPU. Logs are in `logs/`.

### Settings that differ from the paper
- **Data is rebuilt, not the authors' data:** a different random sample of LLM revisions, served by vLLM in bf16 rather than HF fp16 `generate`.
- **Only 2 of the 4 revision LLMs:** DeepSeek-V3.2 and GPT-4o need API keys, which are not available. The "Avg." columns are therefore not computable.
- Seeds 1 and 2 were added; seed 0 is the paper-comparable number.

What the ablations show:
- **EBD (largest claimed effect):** reproduces on Qwen3: +2.1 points rewrite, +1.9 polish over the MCL baseline. On Llama3 every variant is already at about 0.985–0.99, so there is no measurable gain.
- **LW:** helps when EBD is off (+0.3 average, all 4 columns positive). On top of EBD it is mixed: full 0.9613 vs. 0.9620 without LW.
- **CM module vs. direct connection:** a tie (0.9613 vs. 0.9616).
- These LW and CM differences (≤0.002) are smaller than the seed-to-seed std of the full model (0.0006–0.0055).
- So the paper's ordering is reproduced for EBD, but **not** resolvable for LW-on-top-of-EBD or for CM (the paper's own gaps are 0.0024 and 0.0014).

## Why not "reproduced"
1. **Missing data (main reason).** The paper's own generated datasets (the LLM revisions) are not released, only the script. Our datasets are rebuilt by stochastic sampling (T=0.7), so the numbers cannot be compared directly, even though they are close.
2. **External blocker.** No DeepSeek/OpenRouter API keys, so half of the LLM settings could not be rebuilt.
3. **No baselines in the release.**

## Codebase completeness: `MISSING_DATA`
The method and the main train/eval pipeline are complete and ran without any source edit. `git diff` in `./codebase` is empty; the only change was deleting `__pycache__`. Notes:
- **Data:** the paper's generated datasets are not released. Rebuilt the Qwen3-32B and Llama-3-8B-Instruct CSVs with `scripts/gen_vllm.py`, a vLLM batched equivalent of `data/makedata.py` that imports its prompts.
- **API keys:** `data/makedata.py` has empty key placeholders for GPT-4o (OpenRouter) and DeepSeek.
- **Dependencies:** `requirements.txt` pins `openai==1.95.1`, which conflicts with vLLM 0.10.0; we used 1.90.0. We used torch 2.7.1+cu126 instead of +cu118. Both are environment-only.
- **Config:** `train_config.py` ships `data_file=[""]`. `scripts/run_lamcl.py` sets data_file, SEED and save_path, which are config values only.
- **Ablations:** the release has no ablation switches (Table 4/10). We wrote a patched copy, `scripts/ablation_code/LAMCL.py`.
- **Missing scripts:** the release has no Fig. 5 length-split evaluation (we wrote `scripts/eval_len.py`), no baselines, and no cross-scenario evaluation.

| code_vs_paper | `loss.py` `MCL.forward` | Eq. 17: InfoNCE / softmax contrastive loss | `BCEWithLogitsLoss` over [pos, hard-negs] similarity logits (target 1 for the positive); in-batch BCE before the queue is ¼ full | yes | major |
