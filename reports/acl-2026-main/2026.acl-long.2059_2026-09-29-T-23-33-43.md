**Summary.**
- **PM's own numbers reproduce well.**
  - MME hallucination subset (existence/count/position/color): 190 / 170 / 133.33 / 170, total **663.33**. The paper reports 195 / 175 / 138.33 / 175, total 683.33. Every subtask is exactly −5, a relative gap of 2.9%.
  - POPE: all 9 splits are within 0.63 pt of Table 5. The average is **86.41** vs 86.70.
  - MME-Cognition: 3 of the 4 subtasks match exactly (117.86 / 42.5 / 107.5). Code reasoning is 72.5 vs 87.5, giving a total of 340.36 vs 355.36.
- **The claimed improvement over greedy decoding is much smaller than in the paper.** The un-magnified baseline from the same released pipeline is stronger than the paper's "Greedy" row: MME 648.33 vs 630.00, POPE AVG 85.96 vs 84.59. This baseline is either run separately, or read from the first (un-magnified) pass stored inside the PM run; the two give identical numbers.
  - MME: PM's gain is **+15.0** vs +53.3 in the paper.
  - POPE AVG: the gain is **+0.45** vs +2.11. PM beats greedy on 7 of 9 splits (it loses on AOKVQA-popular and GQA-adversarial).
  - The direction of the hallucination claim holds.
- **The "PM does not hurt cognition" claim is not reproduced.** PM scores 340.36 vs 355.71 for greedy.
- **The Table 3 ablation ordering only partly holds.** Full PM beats "w/o IR" and "w/o MLA", but "w/o IR & MLA" (663.33) ties full PM. The ablation flags are my guess.
- **Overall result: MIXED.** The per-number values match, the main direction holds with a much smaller margin, and one secondary claim fails.

## What I ran

Model: `liuhaotian/llava-v1.5-7b` (fp16, HF cache), with transformers 4.37.2 and torch 2.1.2+cu121. Hardware: 1× H200. The paper used an RTX 3090. All runs use the released `experiments/eval/model_generate.py`.

- **MME, PM** (flags identical to `experiments/scripts/mme/run_llava_pm.sh`):
  `scripts/run_mme.sh pm --image_resample --map_constructor sum_scale --heatmap iterative --iterative_search token_mask --iterative_regularization --exit_threshold 0.3 --normalize_mask norm_enhance --enhance_coe 10 --kernel_size 3 --exit_criteria sum_attention`
  The run covers 500 questions: the four hallucination subtasks plus the four cognition subtasks. That is what the code filters to.
- **POPE, PM** (flags identical to `experiments/scripts/pope/run_llava_pm.sh`: α=15, k=5, β=0.4, seed 55): `scripts/run_pope.sh pm <aokvqa|gqa|coco> <adversarial|popular|random> ...`, 9 × 3000 questions.
- **POPE, PM with the paper-text hyperparameters** (α=10, k=3, β=0.3), all 9 splits: stored as method `pm_papercfg`. Result: AOKVQA 80.37/87.47/91.87 (avg 86.57), GQA 81.27/84.33/90.47 (avg 85.36), COCO 85.33/88.10/89.07 (avg 87.50), AVG **86.48**. This is essentially identical to the script config (86.41), so the α/k/β discrepancy does not matter for POPE.
- **Greedy**: the same command with no PM flags (`scripts/run_mme.sh greedy`, `scripts/run_pope.sh greedy ...`). The answer is the argmax of the first-token yes/no probability, which is what the released evaluators use. It gives the same accuracy on every POPE split and the same MME scores as the `original_p_y` first pass recorded inside the PM runs.
- **Table 3 ablations** (MME): w/o IR = `--heatmap attention`; w/o MLA = `--single_layer`; w/o both = both flags. The other flags are as for PM.
- **Table 4(b)** (MME, iterative PM map + a different prompting method): `--resample_method blurring|rectangle|api|crop` with default thresholds.
- **Evaluation**:
  - `scripts/eval_mme.py <method>` wraps the released `experiments/eval/MME/convert_and_eval.py` (score = acc + acc+).
  - `scripts/eval_pope.py <method>` uses the same rule as `eval_pope_seeds.py`: answer yes iff `logits_score[0] > logits_score[1]`.
  - `scripts/build_comparisons.py` and `scripts/write_report.py` build the comparison table below.
- Outputs:
  - raw generations: `ckpt/MME/llava/*/` and `ckpt/POPE/*/`
  - scores: `results/*.json`
  - logs: `logs/`

**Settings that differ from the paper:**
- GPU: H200 instead of RTX 3090. fp16 numerics could flip a few borderline MME questions; each MME question is worth 2.5–5 points on these small subsets.
- Several jobs (3–7) shared one GPU concurrently. This does not change the computation.
- The paper hyperparameters for POPE are ambiguous (see Discrepancies), so I ran both settings.
- Nothing was scaled down.

## Why this is not a full reproduction
1. **Closed judge.** LLaVA-Bench (Table 2, GPT-4o judge) and MMHal-Bench (Fig. 3, GPT-4 judge) need the OpenAI API, and no key is configured. MMHal data is also not part of the release. These were not run (`CLOSED_JUDGE_UNAVAILABLE`).
2. **Baselines not released.** The repository implements PM plus some map/prompting variants. It has no code or configs for OPERA, VCD, ICD, SID, VDD, M3ID, PAI, IBD, or standalone API/ViCrop. The "PM beats method X" claims in Tables 1, 5 and 6 are therefore NOT_COMPARABLE, except against greedy.
3. **Greedy baseline mismatch.** The released pipeline's own un-magnified pass is stronger than the paper's Greedy row. As a result the paper's headline margins (+53 on MME, +2.1 on POPE) shrink to +15 and +0.45.
4. **Cognition.** PM loses 15 points of MME-Cognition against greedy in our run, all on code_reasoning (72.5 vs 87.5 in the paper). The paper states PM does not affect cognition.
5. Shikra (Table 7) was not attempted; the release contains no Shikra integration.

## Discrepancies
- **code_vs_paper** / minor — *experiments/scripts/pope/run_llava_pm.sh vs Sec. 4 "Implementation Details"*. Paper: alpha (enhance coefficient) = 10, kernel size k = 3, IR threshold beta = 0.3 (single setting stated for the experiments). Code/other: POPE script uses --enhance_coe 15 --kernel_size 5 --exit_threshold 0.4 (MME script uses 10 / 3 / 0.3 as in the paper). Affects results: True.
- **code_vs_paper** / minor — *experiments/eval/model_generate.py (norm_enhance branch -> API_LLaVA.main.invtrans with Image.BICUBIC)*. Paper: Sec. 3.1: uniform smoothing then *bilinear* upsampling of the token-level heatmap to the pixel-level perception map P. Code/other: the 24x24 map is upsampled with PIL BICUBIC interpolation (bilinear is only used for the image resampling step). Affects results: True.
- **code_vs_paper** / minor — *model_generate.py iterative loop (deep_mean = deep_ori_aggregate / (n_layers - deep_layer)); exit_criteria sum_attention*. Paper: Eq. 1 / Alg. 1: H is the *sum* over layers l >= L of the head-max attention; stop when the total attention of H(i) < beta. Code/other: for iterative refinement and the stopping test the layer-sum is divided by the number of aggregated layers (i.e. a layer mean), so beta is a threshold on the mean-over-layers attention mass. Affects results: False.
- **code_vs_paper** / major — *Table 1 / Table 5 "Greedy" rows vs released pipeline*. Paper: Greedy LLaVA-1.5-7B: MME hallucination subset 630.00, POPE AVG 84.59 (e.g. AOKVQA popular 84.93, COCO random 86.80). Code/other: no greedy/baseline script is released; running the released model_generate.py without --image_resample (and, identically, the un-magnified first pass stored as original_p_y inside the PM run) gives 648.33 on MME and 85.96 POPE AVG (AOKVQA popular 87.30, COCO random 88.27). The protocol behind the paper's greedy numbers is not in the release, and the gap roughly halves-to-quarters the claimed PM gains. Affects results: True.
- **code_vs_paper** / minor — *model_generate.py __main__ (args.temperature = 1.0 hard-coded; do_sample=True)*. Paper: PM samples the next token from softmax(O^) ; Table 1 compares against greedy decoding. Code/other: the CLI --temperature is overridden to 1.0 and generation samples; for MME/POPE the evaluators score the first-token yes/no probabilities (naive / logits_score), so the reported metrics are argmax (greedy-equivalent) and the sampled "text" field is ignored. Affects results: False.
- **paper_internal** — *Appendix A vs Section 4 "Datasets and Baselines"*. Paper: Section 4: "We evaluate our method on four benchmarks: MME, POPE, MMHal-Bench and LLaVA-Bench". Code/other: Appendix A: "We carry out our experiments on three benchmarks" (then describes four). Affects results: False.
