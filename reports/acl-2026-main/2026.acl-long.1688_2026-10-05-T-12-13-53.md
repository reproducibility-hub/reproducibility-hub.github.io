## Summary
The released code ran **without any source edits**: synthetic KG data generator → GPT-2-style pretraining from
scratch → QA SFT → evaluation via `run_pipeline.sh`, with only environment-variable overrides. I trained 8 models from
scratch (N ∈ {2k, 5k, 10k, 20k, 50k} at K=10, and K ∈ {1, 3, 5} at N=10k; all L=12). For each I evaluated all 14
evaluation files with the released evaluator.

**What reproduces**
* **Phase transition of in-context generalization (completion, ICL-C) with N** (Fig. 1a, Sec. 4.2). ICL-C goes from
  0% at N=2000 to 13.8/29.0% at N=5000 (paper App. A.4.2: 18/34%), 56/73% at N=10000, 93.2/98.2% at N=20000 and 95.2/99.2% at N=50000.
  Values are inversion/symmetry. During training, ICL-C at N=50000 is already 97–100% after 2k iterations, while it stays 0 at N=2000.
* **Memorization / logic at small N** (Sec. 3, Sec. 4.2). At N=2000: Memorize-people 47.1% (paper ≈50%),
  Memorize-job 96.5% (paper >95%), Logic forward inversion 92.4% and symmetry 97.6% (paper >95%), ICL-C and ICL-Q ≈0
  (paper ≈0). Logic QA decreases as N grows (inversion forward 92→91→86→79% from N=2k→5k→10k→20k).
* **Order-bias explanation of reversal failures** (Sec. 5, Fig. 1b/8). Reverse queries are far below forward ones for
  memorize-people (99.1 vs 47.1) and for logic QA (inversion 92.4 vs 40.6, symmetry 97.6 vs 43.4). Within a format,
  inversion ≈ symmetry. Bidirectionally-trained job facts have forward ≈ reverse (96.7 vs 96.5). The same pattern
  holds at N=5000 and N=10000.
* **Layer-wise signature** (Fig. 6, 10, 13; own logit-lens script, since none was released). At N=2000 the gold-token
  rank stays at 100–200 through layer 8, improves at layers 9–11 (prob up to 19%), then collapses at the last layer
  (rank 30→135). At N=5000/10000/50000 the rank drops sharply from layer ~6 and reaches ≈1–2 by the final layer
  with no final-layer collapse, matching the paper's description.
* **Template diversity affects memorization** (Fig. 4b, App. A.4.1). At N=10000, memorization rises monotonically
  with K (people-rev 0.7/26.8/35.2/42.3%, job-rev 2.5/49.7/68.1/81.7% for K=1/3/5/10).

**What does not reproduce**
* **Table 1 (effect of K on ICL-C at N=10000) is reversed.** Ours, inversion/symmetry: K=1 91.7/93.0, K=3 95.2/97.1,
  K=5 77.3/93.9, K=10 56.2/73.4. Paper: 13.5/18.7, 30.2/52.0, 66.6/89.6, 95.0/97.0. Low template diversity does
  *not* prevent in-context generalization in our runs. The K=10 run never exceeded ~73% inversion on our snapshots
  (70.7/66.0/61.7/73.0% at 2k/5k/10k/15k iterations, final 56.2%), so the gap is not only a matter of picking a different epoch.
* **ICL-QA (QA-based in-context generalization) emergence at large N** (Sec. 4.2/4.3: transition above N=20k,
  reaching ~85%). With the paper's SFT recipe (constant LR 3e-5, 32,768 tok/iter, 3,000 iters for N=50000,
  Table 3), the N=50000 model **never learns the QA format**. SFT loss starts at ~18 and is still 2.5 at the end
  (vs 0.16 for N=10000). Its outputs are degenerate and every post-SFT metric is ≈0% (ICL-QA, memorize, logic).
  Continuing SFT to 9,000 iterations (val loss 1.69) still gives ≈0%. Restarting SFT with a fresh optimizer follows
  the same loss curve. The N=50000 pretrained model has a very large logit scale (mean final-layer gold logit ≈52,
  vs 12 at N=2000), which plausibly explains the slow adaptation. ICL-QA at N≤10000 is ≤4% (K=10), consistent with
  the paper's "no transition below 20k", but the claimed rise above 20k could not be observed.
  At N=20000 (20k iterations) ICL-QA is still low (forward 5.6/4.8%) while ICL-C is 93.2/98.2%.

## What was run

All main runs use the released `run_pipeline.sh` **without any source edits**, launched by `runs/queue.sh`
(one copy of the code per run under `runs/<name>/`, because `train.py` hard-codes `data/` and the pipeline
rewrites config files in place). Only environment variables were set:

```
PY=env/bin/python NPROC=1 N=<N> K=<K> N_LAYER=12 PRETRAIN_ITERS=<iters> SFT_ITERS=<2000|3000> \
BATCH_SIZE=48 GRAD_ACCUM=10 FT_GRAD_ACCUM=32 RUN_EVAL=false bash run_pipeline.sh   # datagen+pretrain+SFT
RUN_DATAGEN=false RUN_PRETRAIN=false RUN_SFT=false RUN_EVAL=true bash run_pipeline.sh  # evaluation
```

* Hardware: 1×H200 instead of 8×A100. Pretraining batch kept at the paper's 491,520 tokens/iter
  (48 micro-batch × 1024 ctx × 10 accumulation; the paper used 12×1024×40 across 8 GPUs), SFT batch at 32,768
  tokens/iter (1×1024×32), LR 6e-4 cosine→6e-5 with 500 warmup (pretrain), constant 3e-5 (SFT), bf16,

## Why not fully reproduced
* **Results.** The Table 1 template-diversity effect is reversed in our runs, and the ICL-QA emergence at large N
  fails because SFT does not converge at N=50000 with the released/paper SFT recipe. I found no code bug that
  explains either. The main code/paper differences (an extra composite sentence in the pretraining paragraphs, and
  15 instead of 7 SFT QA pairs per sample, including forward-format questions) are listed under Discrepancies; their
  effect is unknown.
* **Compute / scope.** The paper uses 8×A100, best-epoch selection per configuration, and many sweeps (E, K, N up to
  200k, L from 1 to 30+). Within 48 h on one H200 I ran single-seed final-checkpoint runs on the Table-3 schedules for
  N=2k/5k/10k/50k, a K sweep at N=10k, and N=20k at a reduced schedule.
* **Not released.** The diffusion baseline (README says it is not included), the layer-wise analysis code, the
  pretrained-GPT-2 continuation experiment (Table 2) and the compositional-relation generator (Sec. 6.2). Those
  claims were not checked, except the layer-wise one, which I checked with my own script.

## Discrepancies
**code_vs_paper**
1. *Major* — `data/synthetic_data_generation.py::generate_example_1`. Paper: Train Graphs have 7 triples/sentences.
   Code: each train paragraph has an 8th composite sentence ("the {father|uncle|wife} of n1 works as j2" etc.).
2. *Major* — the SFT corpus (`ft_qa_pair_data`). Paper: 7 QA pairs per train sample, one per triple. Code: 15 per sample,
   including forward-format and reverse-format questions for both relation pairs, both job formats, and a composite
   job question. SFT therefore exposes both query orders.
3. *Minor* — `run_pipeline.sh` defaults (PRETRAIN_ITERS=5000; GRAD_ACCUM=5×NPROC) differ from the Table 3 schedules and
   the paper's batch on fewer than 8 GPUs.
4. *Minor* — `test/evaluate_model.py` scoring (not specified in the paper). Case-insensitive substring match of the gold
   answer anywhere in one sampled continuation of ≤500 tokens. For ICL completion the model writes a whole
   paragraph, so the gold name can match anywhere in it.
5. *Minor* — question templates are lowercase ("who …?", "what …?"); the paper shows "Who/What".

**paper_internal**
6. Table 3 lists the best pretraining length for N=20000 as 1400 epochs (×36.6 iters/epoch ≈ 51k iterations). Sec.
   4.4 / Fig. 7 say ICL-C for N=20000 jumps to near-perfect "around E≈20", with panels of "early and later 30
   training epochs". The unit of E is inconsistent; I used Table 3 as epochs.
