## Why it is not a full reproduction
- Compute: the Qwen3-0.6B Text-to-LoRA hypernetwork is not released; the paper trained it ~2 days on 4×A100. We re-trained it with the
  released T2L code for half the steps on one H200 (coverage SCALED_DOWN). Per-Pcs and PriME baselines were not run (time), so claims
  "PRISP beats Per-Pcs/PriME" are NOT_COMPARABLE; full-data Table 3 not run.
- Evaluation as released cannot produce the paper's classification/rating numbers (no answer extraction); our extraction is a guess.
- LaMP-1 (125 queries) and LaMP-7 (114 queries) are small and generation is sampled, so per-task differences of a few points are noise-level.
