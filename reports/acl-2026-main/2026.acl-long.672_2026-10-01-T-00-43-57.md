## Summary
- We ran all of Table 1: 6 methods (CycleShift, DepthW, StealthInk, MPAC, RSBH, XMark) × 3 tasks (C4 text completion, CNN/DailyMail summarization, WritingPrompts story generation) × T ∈ {150, 200, 250, 300}. - **The main claim holds.** In our runs XMark has the highest bit accuracy (BA) in all 12 task/T columns of Table 1. On C4 at T=300 it ties CycleShift, both at 100%.
- **Gains over MPAC match the paper.** Averaged over T, XMark beats MPAC by +5.62 points on summarization (paper: +5.81) and +6.63 on story generation (paper: +6.81).

  - On C4, XMark has lower PPL than CycleShift, MPAC and RSBH and higher than StealthInk, as the paper says.
  - One small difference: in our runs DepthW is slightly below XMark on C4 (3.90 vs 3.97), whereas the paper has them essentially tied (4.64 vs 4.61).
  - On the chat tasks, XMark stays close to unwatermarked PPL: +0.16 on CNN/DM (paper +0.07) and +0.24 on WritingPrompts (paper +0.34).
  - We found no code or setting difference that explains the absolute offset. Perplexity uses the LLaMA-2-13B oracle, exactly as in the release.

## Discrepancies
- **code_vs_paper (minor; affects baseline results): DepthW green-list ratio.**
  - Paper (Sec 4.1): DepthW's green-list ratio is 0.5.
  - Code: `processor_detector.py` builds `DepthWProcessor`/`DepthWDetector` with `gamma=args.gamma`, whose default is 0.25. RSBH is correctly hard-coded to 0.5.
  - We ran the code as released (γ=0.25).
- **Absolute PPL offset (not counted as a discrepancy).** PPL for both watermarked and unwatermarked text is consistently lower than in the paper. The code's PPL procedure (13B oracle, loss over response tokens given the prompt) is consistent with the paper's description, and we found no code/paper difference that explains it. Possible causes include the generation details or code version the authors used; we can't verify this.
- **Paper internal consistency.** We checked the text's numbers against Table 1 (averages, +5.81/+6.81 gains, +3.5 over CycleShift, the 0.07/0.34 PPL gaps). All are consistent.
