None of the MTEB sentiment datasets changed on the Hub after mid-2025, so the cause is unknown. It may be a library-version difference in tokenisation or sampling. It does not change any ranking or conclusion.
- The paper does not say whether Table 3 uses whitened embeddings. Our base (non-whitened) LUNAR numbers match it, so we compare base.
- LUNAR/AE/IF are stochastic, so ±1–3 points per dataset is expected (e.g. Table 3 SMS XLM-R: 68.90 vs 65.40).
- Not reported in the paper: on NLP-ADBench, whitening before LUNAR helps on spam/Yelp but hurts on some news datasets (e.g. Qwen3-E N24News 83.7 → 77.2).
