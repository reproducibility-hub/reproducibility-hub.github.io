## 1. What the paper claims and what was targeted

The main experiment is intent classification: Table 4, with the derived differences in Table 6. Models are trained on German (MASSIVE text / Speech-MASSIVE audio) and evaluated on German and Bavarian (MASSIVE de-ba text, xSID text/audio). There are three setups:

- **text-only**: 4 text encoders.
- **cascaded**: the same text models applied to ASR transcripts from 9 ASR models.
- **speech-only**: 9 speech encoders fine-tuned on audio.

The supporting topic-classification experiment uses SwissDial (text-only and cascaded). The three claimed findings (§1):

1. Cascaded systems lag behind text-only on German. With a normalising ASR (Whisper large), they beat text-only on dialect input (§5.1).
2. Speech-only is on par with cascaded on German but greatly outperforms cascaded on Bavarian (§5.2: "across the board, +5.6 to 17.9 pp").

### 3.1 Training collapses (main source of deviations)

Several runs never left the majority class: dev accuracy stayed at 36.6 % for every epoch, and they predict a single label at test time.

- **XLM-R large, intent, lr 1e-4** (the paper's chosen LR): seeds 1234 and 2345 collapsed; seed 3456 was fine (xSID deu 91.7 / bar 68.7). I ran 3 extra seeds (4567/5678/6789; not in the tables): 5678 collapsed, the other two were fine. **3 of 6 runs collapse.** The paper's Table 11 reports this configuration as stable (dev 96.9 ± 0.2), and the released predictions show no collapse.
- **mHuBERT, intent, lr 5e-5**: seed 1234 collapsed. A diagnostic run with the exact released config (batch 32, no accumulation, label seed 8888) trained normally (xSID 59.7 / 50.7; paper 59.7 / 53.6). The collapse is therefore seed-dependent and not caused by gradient accumulation.

Because the paper pools all runs into its aggregate rows, these collapses dominate several aggregates. For the claims I therefore also report a variant **excluding the 3 collapsed runs** ("excl.").

## 5. Why it is not fully reproduced

1. **Training instability not visible in the paper.**
   - XLM-R large (intent, lr 1e-4) collapses to the majority class in 3 of 6 seeds; mHuBERT in 1 of 4 runs.
   - The authors' released predictions contain no such collapse. Their Table 11 also shows this LR as stable for XLM-R large (96.9 ± 0.2 dev), but very unstable for XLM-R large on topics (34.7 ± 23.5 at 5e-5).
   - I found no bug or config difference that explains it. Same transformers version; the README says the seeds were not set reproducibly.
2. **Whisper large-v3 speech-only is clearly weaker on Bavarian** (63.5 vs 72.5), although dev accuracy is in line with Table 11. A diagnostic run with the exact released config (batch 32, fp32) recovers the German number (83.0 vs 82.7) but not the Bavarian one (65.5), so the gap is not explained by my deviations (§3.6).
3. **Gated data:** the speech-only MASSIVE-deu column (Speech-MASSIVE-test) could not be evaluated. This is external to the release.

## 7. Discrepancies

- **D1 (code_vs_paper, minor, affects results).**
  - App. D says the maximum number of epochs for Whisper large-v3 (intent) is 15.
  - The released predictions behind Table 4 were produced with `maxep30` (filenames `openai-whisper-large-v3_maxep30_bs32_lr1_*`), and the README gives no large-v3 intent command.
  - I used 30.
- **D2 (code_vs_paper, minor, affects results slightly).**
  - In `evaluate_intents.py`, the glob `{text_model}*{seed}*{asr_model}*{test}` with `asr_model="openai-whisper-large-v3"` also matches `openai-whisper-large-v3-turbo` files. Which file is used depends on file-system order.
  - The paper's Whisper large-v3 cascaded row is 92.2 / 80.8 / 61.4 (Table 4) and +2.6 for ∆ casc.−text xSID-bar (Table 6). Correctly aggregating the released predictions gives 92.3 / 80.8 / 61.6 and +2.8.
- **D3 (code_vs_paper, minor, does not affect the paper's numbers).**
  - The released `scores/intents/intent_scores_acc-by-setup-type.tsv` pools the 6 ASR-trained text models (App. E) into the "Cascaded" aggregates (N = 270; N = 30 per ASR model). Example: XLS-R 300M DE 87.2, Whisper tiny 80.4.
  - Paper Table 4 uses only the 4 gold-trained models (N = 108 / 12; 88.0, 79.8).
- **D4 (paper_internal, minor).**
  - Footnote 9 lists "XLS-R300M – XLM-Rlg: −4.7, XLS-R300M – XLM-Rlg: +7.0". The +7.0 is XLS-R 300M **DE** − XLM-R large (66.0 − 59.0).
  - §3.3 says "758 hidden dimensions" for the ~90M models; it should be 768.
