## 1. What the paper claims

The paper defines two reference-free metrics computed from token log-probs:
* **CCI** (Eq. 1/3): `log2 Σ_q P(a_t|q,a_<t)P(q) − log2 Σ_q P(a_≤t|q)P(q)`. The sum over `q` runs over the questions in the same evaluation batch.
* **QACI** (Eq. 5): `log2 P(answer|q) − log2 P(answer)`.

It then makes these empirical claims:
1. **Table 1** (CNN/DM, T5-small/base/large, Mistral-7B-v0.1, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Llama-3-8B): Mistral has the highest CCI and T5-large the highest QACI. CCI correlates negatively with Topic-JS (r = −0.59), weakly positively with Cosine (0.16) and weakly with Jaccard (−0.29). QACI correlates strongly with ROUGE-L (0.80), chrF (0.66) and TER (−0.77).
2. **Fig. 2/3** (exclusivity): CCI is above PMI at every position. PMI's speed keeps increasing with positive acceleration. CCI's speed rises early, and its acceleration starts negative and goes to about 0.
3. **Table 3**: the CCI mean rises slightly with |Q| (13.30 → 16.05), and the change is small next to the std (~10.4–11).
4. **Fig. 4/5/6** (fine-tuning T5-base and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct on CNN/DM for 5 epochs): CCI rises sharply from epoch 0 to 1 and then fluctuates. The IQR shrinks. QACI rises (for Qwen there is a dip from epoch 0 to 1). CCI rises with relative token position.
5. **Table 2 / CMRC 2018**: the mean CCI per relative-position interval is 2.26 / 3.45 / 4.54 / 5.32 / 6.34. About 92 % of samples have a positive CCI slope. QACI correlates with answer quality (Pearson 0.72, Spearman 0.74).
6. **Fig. 7**: CCI increases monotonically with question difficulty level. **Fig. 8**: QACI increases monotonically with question-length level (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, released question pools).

## 2. What the release contains

* `cci_computation.py`: `compute_cci()`, a correct implementation of Eq. 3. Its variable names are swapped relative to the paper ("denominator_terms" holds the paper's numerator), but the returned value equals Eq. 3.
* `qaci_computation.py`: `compute_qaci()`, plus the two demo drivers `run_cci.py` and `run_qaci.py`. Each driver hard-codes a model path of `/path/to/your/model` and one or two questions.
* `run_pmi.py`: the PMI baseline over the difficulty question pool (first 10 questions per level).
* `Qwen-train.py` and `T5-base-train.py`: fine-tuning scripts.
* Question pools `difficulty_levels/level{1..5}.json` (200 questions per level) and `length_levels/level{1..5}.json` (100 per level). All questions are in Chinese.
* **Not included:** any pipeline for Table 1 (CNN/DM generation, the Cosine/Jaccard/Topic-JS/ROUGE/chrF/TER metrics, the repeats, the correlations), for CMRC 2018, for evaluating fine-tuning checkpoints (Fig. 4–6), for Table 3, or for Fig. 7/8 aggregation. There is no CCI driver over a dataset either: `run_cci.py` is a 2-question demo.

### 4.3 Summary of claim-level outcomes

**Holds (TREND_MATCH):**
* **Fig. 8:** QACI rises monotonically with question-length level, more steeply at the top levels (83 → 98 → 113 → 137 → 175 bits). This needs our fixed QACI. The released QACI code gives negative, non-monotonic values (−321 … −353).
* **Fig. 7:** CCI rises with difficulty (30.3, 43.3, 43.0, 49.3, 50.2). Levels 2 and 3 are tied, so the increase is not strictly monotonic.
* **Fig. 2/3:** CCI is above PMI at every position. PMI's speed grows (0.44 → 1.6 bits per step) with positive, decaying acceleration. CCI's speed is high from the start, with slightly negative acceleration early and about 0 later.
* **Table 3:** the CCI mean rises mildly with |Q| (+0.9, +1.2, +0.9 per step; the paper has +0.8, +1.1, +0.85). The rise is small next to the std (11.2; the paper's is about 10.4–11.0). Our absolute level differs (42–45 vs 13–16) because the paper does not say which data Table 3 uses.
* **CMRC:** CCI increases over relative-position intervals, and 90.9 % of samples have a positive slope (paper: about 92 %).
* **Table 1:** Mistral-7B has the highest CCI.
* **Table 1:** ROUGE-L, chrF and TER for T5-small and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, plus TER for Mistral and T5-large, land within tolerance of the paper. This suggests our generation set-up is close to the authors'.
* **Fig. 5:** QACI rises over fine-tuning for T5-base. For Qwen there is a dip from epoch 0 to 1 followed by a rise. The dip-then-rise pattern holds both with the released (buggy) training script and with the EOS-fixed one.
* **Fig. 6:** CCI rises with relative position in every epoch.

**Does not hold:**
* **CMRC:** QACI is *negatively* correlated with answer quality. Pearson is −0.41 and Spearman −0.45, where the paper reports +0.72 / +0.74. With a "short answer" prompt it is still −0.10 / −0.26. The cause is that QACI is a sum over answer tokens: it tracks answer length (r = 0.90), and over-long extractions get lower F1. Normalising by length gives only r = +0.25.
* **Table 1, QACI vs. reference metrics:** the per-sample QACI correlations are weak. Against ROUGE-L we get 0.18 (paper 0.80); against chrF −0.07 (paper 0.66); against TER −0.34 (paper −0.77). Correlating the 6 model averages gives ROUGE-L 0.72 but chrF −0.79, the opposite sign to the paper.
* **Table 1:** T5-large is not the top-QACI model; it ties with T5-small. The individual CCI and QACI values per model do not match (CCI within about 10–25 %).
* **Fig. 4 (T5-base):** CCI does *not* rise from epoch 0 to 1; it falls from 18.1 to 15.5.
* **Fig. 4 (Qwen):** CCI does rise sharply at epoch 1, from 15.0 to 33.6. But with the released training script part of that rise comes from the model no longer emitting EOS, so every output hits the 128-token limit. With EOS fixed, CCI goes 15.0 → 20.0 and then declines steadily to 9.1, which is not "minor fluctuation". The Qwen CCI IQR also widens at epoch 1 instead of contracting.

Overall result: **MIXED.** The qualitative dynamic claims about the metrics (Fig. 2/3, 7, 8, Table 3, and the CMRC position trend) mostly hold. The external-validity claims (QACI's strong correlation with reference metrics and with answer quality) did not reproduce, and some fine-tuning trends did not either.

**Result as released: DOES_NOT_RUN.**
* There are no scripts for any main experiment.
* `run_pmi.py` crashes with a `KeyError` on the released `difficulty_levels/level5.json`.
* The released QACI driver computes a different quantity. On Fig. 8 it gives negative, non-monotonic values (shown in the last column of §4.2).

## 5. Why it is not fully reproduced

1. **Missing pipelines.** The paper's settings are under-specified for everything except the metric formulas:
   * the summarisation prompts for the base models;
   * the generation length;
   * the |Q| pool used for CCI in Table 1, CMRC and Fig. 7;
   * the implementations of Cosine, Jaccard and Topic-JS (embedding model, sentence unit, topic model);
   * the correlation protocol;
   * the model, prompt and quality metric for CMRC;
   * the data for Table 3.

   We had to choose all of these ourselves, so absolute values are not comparable.
2. **The released QACI code is wrong (D1),** so the QACI definition used for the paper's numbers is unknown. In particular we cannot tell what context gives `P(answer)`; we used a BOS-only context.
3. **The released Qwen fine-tuning script drops EOS (D2).** This changes the generations, and with them CCI/QACI, after fine-tuning.
4. **Compute:** T5-base fine-tuning used 20 % of the CNN/DM train set instead of all of it. On a shared GPU the full set would have taken about 6 h per epoch. The Qwen run used the paper's 20 % for 5 epochs.

## 6. Codebase completeness: MISSING_SCRIPTS

The metric functions (`compute_cci`, `compute_qaci`), the PMI script and the fine-tuning scripts are released. Every experiment pipeline behind the paper's tables and figures is missing. **No file in `./codebase` was edited** (`git status` is clean); all fixes live in copies under `repro/`.

Changes and fixes, relative to the release:
* **QACI (`qaci_computation.py` / `run_qaci.py`): wrong as released.** `token_level_log_prob(..., prompt_len=0)` slices `[-1:]`, so the unconditional term scores only the *last* answer token. In addition, `run_qaci.py` decodes `out[0]` including the prompt, so the "answer" contains the system prompt and the question. My `causal_lib.qaci_from_lps` instead scores every answer token both under the prompt and under a BOS-only context (Eq. 5). This is a result-changing fix.
* **CCI:** re-implemented in vectorised form in `causal_lib.py`. It reproduces the released `compute_cci` to within bf16 noise (`results/open_ended/equivalence.json`). This is a speed change only.
* **`run_pmi.py`** crashes because `difficulty_levels/level5.json` stores its list under the key `difficulty_level4`. My loader ignores the key.
* **`T5-base-train.py`** (copied to `repro/T5-base-train_scaled.py`):
  * imported `AdamW` from `torch.optim` instead of transformers;
  * used the dataset id `abisee/cnn_dailymail`;
  * reduced to 20 % of train and 5 epochs (the script has 6);
  * renamed the save directory so it no longer overwrites the `epoch0` it loads from.
* **`Qwen-train.py`** (copied to `repro/Qwen-train_scaled.py`): set the model path to the hub id, set `save_only_model=True`, and made `TRAIN_FRACTION` / `OUT_DIR` configurable through the environment. The eos-fix variant appends `<|im_end|>` to the labels.
* **Model paths:** `run_cci.py` and `run_qaci.py` hard-code `/path/to/your/model`. I imported their prompt and tokenisation helpers instead of running them.
* **README:** it refers to `question_pools/` and `Fine-tune/` directories that do not exist; the files sit at the repo root.
* **Scripts I wrote** (all in `repro/`): `causal_lib.py`, `open_ended.py`, `cnndm_gen_score.py`, `table1_metrics.py`, `table1_corr_variants.py`, `cmrc.py`, `cmrc_short.py`, `ft_summary.py`, `run_table1.sh`, `run_t5ft_eval.sh`, `run_qwenft_eval.sh`, `run_qwenft_eos.sh`, `make_status.py`.

## 8. Settings that differ from (or are unspecified in) the paper

* **Table 1:**
  * Articles: 1000 test articles (seed 42); the paper's 250 / 500 samples per repeat are drawn from them.
  * Decoding: greedy, 128 new tokens; Qwen articles truncated to 900 tokens, T5 inputs to 512.
  * Prompts: base models get a plain instruction prompt (unspecified in the paper).
  * CCI pool |Q| = 10 (unspecified).
  * QACI unconditional context: BOS only (unspecified, released code broken).
  * Cosine = SimCSE sup-roberta-base on adjacent sentences; Topic-JS = LDA with 50 topics on 20k training articles (both unspecified).
  * Models used off the shelf, not fine-tuned (the paper does not say).
* **CMRC:** model (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), prompt, 500 dev questions, and char-F1 as the quality measure are all unspecified in the paper and chosen by us.
* **Table 3:** data unspecified; we used 600 of the released difficulty questions with Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct.
* **Fine-tuning:**
  * T5-base: 20 % of train (scaled down from the full train set used by the script).
  * Qwen: 20 % of train, 5 epochs, as in the script and paper.
  * Evaluation: 500 validation articles, CCI on 250 of them.
* **Precision and hardware:** bf16 for the 7–8B models, 1×H200 (the paper used an A100).
