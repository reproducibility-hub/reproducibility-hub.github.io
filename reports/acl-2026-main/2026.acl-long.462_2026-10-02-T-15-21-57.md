  - E2 is 20-sample forced-choice at T=0.7, not log-probability scoring.
  - Thresholds are empirical interpolation, not logistic fits.
  - Several reported analyses have no code or data.

## 1. What the paper claims and what was targeted
The paper evaluates six LLMs on 1,320 vignettes:
- **E1 (explicit labels):** Table 3 reports strictness P(INAPP), p50 thresholds, range, slope and threshold coverage. Table 15 / Fig. 3 report norm-signature correlations.
- **E2 (implicit acceptability):** run for four models (Claude, LLaMA, Qwen, DeepSeek). Table 4 reports the explicit–implicit gap types: coherent Claude, H-dominant DeepSeek, L-dominant LLaMA.

Models: Claude Sonnet 4, GPT-5-chat and Gemini 2.5 Flash are closed (no API keys configured here). DeepSeek-chat (V3, 671B) and Qwen3-235B-A22B-2507 are open but large. Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct is open.

The repo (https://github.com/GerryFAN0706/feeling_rules) ships:
- the benchmark;
- the inference code (`run_inference_api.py`, OpenAI-compatible);
- the analysis scripts;
- raw outputs: E1 for all six models, and E2 for Qwen and DeepSeek only.

I did two things:
- **(A)** Re-ran the released analysis on the released outputs. This covers all six models.
- **(B)** Re-ran inference with the released script, served by a local vLLM:
  - Llama-3.3-70B in bf16 (also FP8; the two agree on 98.2% of labels);
  - Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507 as a 4-bit AWQ community quantization (`QuantTrio/...-AWQ`). The FP8 or bf16 versions (236 GB+) don't fit on one H200, so the Qwen rerun is only indicative (NOT_COMPARABLE).

Claude, GPT-5 and Gemini could not be rerun (closed models, no keys), and DeepSeek-V3 doesn't fit on one GPU.

## 6. Discrepancies
**Code vs. paper (major):**
- **E1 prompts differ across models and from App. B.**
  - Claude, GPT-5, LLaMA and Qwen use `institutional_binary` ("judge the publicly observable expression", DEPENDS forbidden).
  - DeepSeek uses `institutional` (3-label).
  - Gemini uses `label_only`, a variant of the App. B prompt.
  - The paper presents one prompt for all models. Our reruns show the prompt moves P(INAPP) by 0.1–0.37.
- **E2 is not log-probability scoring.** The released E2 is 20 A/B samples at T=0.7 with p_unacc = share of B. The paper describes summed, length-normalized log p("acceptable") vs. log p("unacceptable") with p = σ(s), obtained via APIs/vLLM. Also, the Anthropic API exposes no logprobs, so the claimed Claude log-prob E2 is not possible as described.
- **Thresholds and range are not logistic fits.** They are empirical linear interpolation over 168 groups (triggers pooled), not monotone logistic fits over 264 groups (Sec. 2.4, App. D). This does match App. G.3.
- **Many reported analyses have no code or data.** Missing: E2 outputs for Claude/LLaMA, the LMM, bootstrap, robustness checks and the human audit.
- **Tables 8/9 can't come from the released outputs.** They report DEPENDS prevalence of 9.8–18.6% for all models. The released E1 has 0 DEPENDS for Claude, GPT-5, LLaMA and Qwen, 3/1320 for DeepSeek, and 12.6% for Gemini.

**Code vs. paper (minor):**
- **Slope:** the code reports one per-model GLM intensity coefficient, not the mean of per-group β_g.
- **Spearman ρ:** the code computes it at the vignette level, not over aggregated cells.

**Paper-internal:**
- **Number of fits:** "1584 curve fits (6×264)" in Sec. 4.1 vs. "n = 7920 fits" in App. D.
- **Threshold definition:** logistic-fit thresholds (Sec. 2.4, App. D) vs. interpolation thresholds (App. G.3).
- **GPT-5 correlations:** recomputed from the released data, GPT-5–LLaMA is 0.695. That would rank Top-2, yet the pair is absent from Table 15, and Fig. 3 shows 0.61. DeepSeek–GPT-5 is 0.385 vs. 0.482 in the paper. The GPT-5 signature in the paper apparently came from a different GPT-5 run than the released one.
- **Mean-centred correlations:** Sec. 4.2 reports separate "mean-centered" correlations (0.72, 0.26), but Pearson r is already invariant to centering a vector.
