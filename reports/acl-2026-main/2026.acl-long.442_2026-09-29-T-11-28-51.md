# Reproduction report — SGPVT (ACL 2026, 2026.acl-long.442)

**Verdict: FAILED** · result match (after fixes): **NO_MATCH** · as released: **DOES_NOT_RUN** · coverage: **SCALED_DOWN** · codebase completeness: **MISSING_SCRIPTS**

In one sentence: the released code runs after a one-line path fix. On the only target whose data was released (Joe Biden, LLaVA-1.5-7B), it matches the paper's utility (GRA/TextVQA) numbers. But the paper's central claim, that the SGPVT retention stream preserves proximal concepts much better than plain gradient ascent (Tab. 1: PRA 75.5 vs 68.5), does not appear. Across 5 paired seeds, SGPVT and GA end up almost the same model: PRA 24.8±9.4 vs 24.0±9.7, and 99% of TextVQA answers are identical. The paper's absolute PRA/FA numbers can't be compared: MMUBench/MMUBench++ is not public, and the released proximal images (Hillary Clinton) aren't one of Biden's listed proximal concepts.

## 1. What the paper claims (targets)
- Tab. 1 / Tab. 2: FA↓ / PRA↑ / GRA↑ for 6 targets × {LLaVA-1.5-7B, LLaVA-1.5-13B, InternVL3-8B} against GA, NPO, GA+KL and FTTP. "Ours" has the best PRA everywhere.
- Tab. 3: component ablation (L_KL, top-K, cosine control). The full model gets PRA 74.9 vs 68.5 with no module.
- Setup (Sec. 4.1): LoRA, 20 steps, AdamW, lr 2e-5, batch 4, T=20, ε=0.05, λk=0.3. Training uses 5–7 forget images; the rest of the 100 images per concept are the FA test set. PRA is measured on 1-hop proximal concepts (Fig. 6; for Joe Biden these are Donald Trump, Nancy Pelosi and Vladimir Putin). GRA is measured on a general benchmark, TextVQA (confirmed by the LLaVA TextVQA defaults in the released `llava/eval/model_vqa.py`).

## 3. What I ran (exact commands)

**Why there is no effect.** In the released trainer the retention loss is the teacher–student cross-entropy averaged over *all* sequence positions: 576 image tokens, the system prompt and the question. That value stays at about 5.5–5.9 nats throughout training, while the GA term drops from about −1 to −8. So the answer-token signal that would protect proximal knowledge is heavily diluted, and training is dominated by GA.

With the same seed, SGPVT and GA models give identical answers on 99–100% of TextVQA questions and on 48–100% of the person-recognition prompts. The Tab. 3-style variants, larger λk and restoring token norms all leave PRA within the GA seed noise.

Qualitatively the unlearned models behave like the paper's case studies: the Biden target gets named as Bush/Reagan, and Hillary gets "a woman, likely a politician". But SGPVT does not fix this any more than GA.

**Overall.**
- result_match = **NO_MATCH**: the method's claimed PRA improvement over GA disappears. Utility (GRA) numbers match, but those are a sanity check.
- result_match_as_released = **DOES_NOT_RUN**: the unmodified code crashes at model construction (`ValueError: Unknown vision tower: /data1/zzj/clip-vit-base-patch16`). The training launcher and scoring code are also missing.
- Coverage **SCALED_DOWN**: 1 of 18 model×target cells, a reduced FA test set, a proxy proximal set, and only GA among the baselines.

## 6. Discrepancies
Code vs. paper:
1. **s_min**: `llava_trainer.py::_perturb_high_attention_embeds_new` hard-codes `s_min = 0.6`. The paper computes it from 100 TextVQA images (Eq. 3); I get 0.34 (pooled CLIP) or 0.89 (patch tokens).
2. **ε**: 0.01 in the code vs 0.05 in the paper.
3. **Token selection**: the paper uses the attention of concept C's textual query to the visual keys (Eq. 2). The code averages the attention from all text tokens after the image (question and answer), over heads and over layers with softmax(linspace(0.1, 2)) weights.
4. **Loss weights**: code defaults are `ga_weight=0.3, kl_weight=0.7`; the README says 1.0/0.5 with top_k 0.1. The paper uses L_UN + 0.3·L_KL, and the ablations use λk=1.2 and top-K 0.2/0.3.
5. **KL**: implemented as −Σ p_teacher log p_student (cross-entropy) averaged over all positions, including image and prompt tokens. `kl_temperature` is defined but unused. This dilution is the likely reason the retention stream has no measurable effect.
6. **Perturbed token norm**: the spherical rotation output is unit norm, while LLaVA visual tokens have norm ≈ 32. This follows the literal Eq. 7 (‖v̂‖=1) but shrinks perturbed tokens about 32×. Restoring the norm didn't change the results.
7. **Hard-coded vision tower**: `/data1/zzj/clip-vit-base-patch16`, incompatible with LLaVA-1.5 (the fix above).
8. **Released data**: 30 Biden images (paper: 100 per concept); no Biden proximal concepts. Only Hillary Clinton is included, who is Trump's proximal concept per Fig. 6. The forget JSON points to `.jpg` files that exist as `.jpeg`.

Paper-internal:
1. **Tab. 1 vs Tab. 3**: Tab. 3's "no module" row (FA 8.0, PRA 68.5) matches Tab. 1's GA row for Biden-7B, but the GRA differs (56.3 vs 55.9). The "full model" row (FA 4.0, PRA 74.9) differs from Tab. 1's "Ours" (FA 6.0, PRA 75.5).
2. **Training steps and λ**: Sec. 4.1 says 20 steps with λk=0.3. Sec. 4.3 says FA reaches 0 at 20 steps, the best balance is at 15 steps, and the ablations use λk=1.2. Eq. 12 calls the weight λk while the text calls it λr.
3. **Curriculum direction**: the text says the curriculum "begins at" the s_min threshold and progressively samples closer proximities. Eq. 4 starts at t=0 at the upper end (≈1) and oscillates.
4. **Metric naming**: Fig. 4's caption says "Target Accuracy (TA)" while the text and tables use FA.
