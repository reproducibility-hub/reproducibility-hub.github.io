**Verdict: PARTIALLY_REPRODUCED**  ·  result match (after fixes): **MIXED**  ·  as released: **DOES_NOT_RUN**  ·  coverage: **SCALED_DOWN**  ·  codebase: **MISSING_SCRIPTS**

## Summary

The paper proposes **self-function vectors (Self-FV)**: the final-token activations of the top-k causal attention heads of the
prompt itself are projected, summed and added back at layer ⌊L/3⌋. The entropy of the resulting prediction is used as the
aleatoric-uncertainty (AU) estimate. The paper evaluates this with Spearman correlations between controlled perturbation levels and the
uncertainty estimates:
- **Table 1** (AU control, WordNetMCQ1/2 example-ratio)
- **Table 2** (AU control, label-flip ratio; 5 datasets × 5 models)
- **Table 3** (EU control, OOD query; a *lower* |ρ| for the AU estimate is better)
- **Table 4** (hallucination-detection AUROC)

The official repo (github.com/LOG-postech/self-fv-icl) contains the full method: CIE head selection, Self-FV and FV
interventions, and entropy logging. It contains no script that sweeps the perturbation levels or computes the Spearman tables, and its
outputs are logged only to wandb. I wrote those pieces (see "Codebase completeness"). I made no edits to the released source files.

**Bottom line.**
- **Table 2 (label-flip AU control) reproduces well.** Across the 12 model/dataset settings run, 6 match the paper's Self-FV number within tolerance:
  - Qwen2.5-7B: WNMCQ1, AG News, Emotion
  - Mistral-7B: WNMCQ1, AG News
  - LLaMA2-7B: AG News

  In all 12 settings, whether Self-FV beats the other methods agrees with the paper.
- **Table 1 (MCQ1/MCQ2 ratio) is mixed.** For LLaMA2-7B the ranking reproduces (Self-FV > FV > TE), with values 0.06–0.07 lower. For LLaMA2-13B, Total Entropy beats Self-FV (0.373 vs 0.304), contradicting the paper.
- **Table 3 (OOD / EU control) largely does not reproduce.** Self-FV has the smallest |ρ| only for LLaMA2-7B. For LLaMA2-13B, Qwen2.5-7B and Mistral-7B it does not, and the magnitudes differ a lot (13B ≈0.31–0.32 vs the paper's 0.08–0.12; Mistral ≈+0.11 vs ≈−0.03). A query-only OOD variant changes which models satisfy the claim. The release does not specify the OOD protocol.
- **Sanity checks:** the parameter-free Total-Entropy baseline matches the paper on 9 of 14 Table 1–2 settings and on 5 of 6 Table 4 AUROCs.
- **Overall: MIXED → PARTIALLY_REPRODUCED.**

## What was run

Models (fp16, 1×H200):
- LLaMA2-7B and LLaMA2-13B (NousResearch mirrors of meta-llama/Llama-2-{7b,13b}-hf; the official repos are gated for this account)
- Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B
- mistralai/Mistral-7B-v0.1

**LLaMA2-70B was not run.** The fp16 weights alone are 140 GB, more than one 141 GB GPU can hold with activations, and the run would not fit the 48 h budget.

Pipeline, per model and dataset (all from `codebase/`, env at `env/`):
1. **Head selection** (`scripts/01_compute_cie.sh` logic, `repro/run_cie.sh` runs the layers in parallel):
   `python main_icl/main.py --model M --data D --answer_type T --num_shots 10 --cie 1 --cie_layer L --layer_end NL --output_dir results/TAG --use_wandb 0` for every layer L, then `python select_top_heads.py --output_dir results/TAG --num_layers NL --top_k 100`.
2. **Perturbation sweep** (`scripts/02_run_self_fv.sh` flags, `repro/run_sweep.sh`): one `main_icl/main.py ... --use_self_fv 1 --use_fv 1 --num_top_heads 20 --seed 42 --cie_seed 24` run per level (interv. layer default ⌊L/3⌋ = 10 / 13 / 9 / 10). Levels:
   - label flip: `--num_noisy_shots k`, k ∈ {0,3,6,9,10} of 10 shots (0/30/60/90/100%, App. D)
   - MCQ1/MCQ2 ratio: `--data wordnet_multi --num_shots 10-k --ood_data wordnet --num_ood_shots k`, k ∈ {0,3,6,9,10}
   - OOD (primary): level 0 `--data wordnet`, level k `--data wordnet_test_oodk`, k=1..5, 10 shots
   - OOD (query-only variant): `--num_shots 0 --ood_data wordnet --num_ood_shots 10`
3. **Evaluation** (`repro/analyze.py`): Spearman ρ between level and per-sample uncertainty, pooled over all test samples × levels (385 WNMCQ1, 202 WNMCQ2, 2000 Emotion, 760 AG News, 1319 GSM8K, 2000 HellaSwag test items). The three uncertainty measures:
   - **Total Entropy:** clean prediction
   - **Function Vector:** FV-intervened prediction
   - **Self-FV:** Self-FV-intervened prediction (= AU estimate)

**Settings that differ from the paper:**
- CIE head selection on the real-world datasets used random train subsets: AG News and Emotion 2000 examples → 200 validation prompts; GSM8K and HellaSwag 1000 → 100. The full 10% split would have been 684–1600 prompts × 1024–1600 heads per model.
- Real-world datasets (Table 2) were run only for LLaMA2-7B (all four) and for Qwen2.5-7B / Mistral-7B (Emotion, AG News). They were not run for LLaMA2-13B.
- **Not run (no code in the release):** the Semantic Entropy and UQ_ICL baselines, the Table 4 hyperparameter grid search, MaxProb / Lookback Lens, and the ablation figures.
- The uncertainty metric (`option_entropy` vs full-vocabulary `entropy`) and the OOD protocol are not specified by the release. I used `option_entropy` because it reproduces the parameter-free Total-Entropy baseline exactly (e.g. Emotion 0.242, AG News 0.739, Qwen WNMCQ1 0.736). Both variants are shown.
- Single seed (42) as in the scripts. The paper reports no std.

## Why it is not fully reproduced

1. **Mixed claims.** Table 2 reproduces (6/12 Self-FV values within tolerance, all 12 consistent in ranking). However, the paper's comparative claims fail in several Table 1/3 settings:
   - Table 1, LLaMA2-13B: Self-FV 0.304 < Total Entropy 0.373 (paper: Self-FV 0.435 best).
   - Table 3, primary protocol: Self-FV does not have the smallest |ρ| for LLaMA2-13B, Qwen2.5-7B or Mistral-7B. For Mistral all three methods correlate positively (≈0.10–0.12), whereas the paper reports ≈ −0.02 to −0.04.
   - Under the query-only OOD variant the outcome flips per model: the claim holds for LLaMA2-13B but not for LLaMA2-7B. The OOD protocol is under-specified in the release.
2. **Missing scripts and ambiguity.** The release does not ship the sweep/Spearman evaluation, so the evaluation protocol was rebuilt from the paper:
   - pooled Spearman over samples × levels
   - levels {0,30,60,90,100}%
   - choice of entropy
   - OOD-level construction

   Remaining differences could come from these choices.
3. **Scaled down.**
   - LLaMA2-70B was not run (fp16 weights don't fit one 141 GB GPU with activations; time).
   - LLaMA2-13B was run on WordNet only.
   - CIE head selection on AG News / Emotion / GSM8K / HellaSwag used 200/200/100/100 validation prompts instead of the full 10% split.
   - Semantic Entropy, UQ_ICL, MaxProb and Lookback Lens baselines are not in the release and were not re-implemented.
   - The Table 4 grid search is not in the release, so the FV/Self-FV AUROCs use default hyperparameters.
4. **Single seed.** The paper gives no variance. Sweeps use seed 42, CIE uses seed 24, as in the scripts.
