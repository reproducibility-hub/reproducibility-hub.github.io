## Target
The main claims are in Table 2 (CodRED, BERT-base, closed and open settings; e.g. closed dev AUC 65.43 / F1 64.58, test AUC 66.47 / F1 64.72), Table 3 (RoBERTa-large), Table 4 (cross-document-only subset), Table 5 (ablations of BIC, Dual-Encoder, GRN and CAO) and Table 7 (CAO added to other backbones). The paper's method has three parts:
- BIC (Bridge-aware Input Construction): scores bridge entities per side with α=5, β=3, γ=1, combines the two sides with a geometric mean, and builds two input streams.
- DSGN: a dual-stream encoder followed by a GRN with L=2 layers.
- CAO (Curriculum-aware Ranking Optimization): curriculum margins (8→10 and 10→8) plus a sigmoid-weighted pairwise ranking loss (λ=0.1, δ=0.3).

- **code_vs_paper (major)**: The CAO loss (Eq. 11–13, App. A) is absent. `debar.py` uses fixed thresholds of 8 and 10 with no ranking penalty.
