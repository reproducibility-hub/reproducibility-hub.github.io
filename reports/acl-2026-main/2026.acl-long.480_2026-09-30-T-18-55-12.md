## What I did
1. **Paper:** read the PDF. The target is Table 1 (main results): BPDMoE-Hate on FHM, MAMI and HarMeme, AUC/ACC, mean ± std over 5 seeds.
2. **Code:** cloned https://github.com/icvplayer/BPDMoE-Hate into `./codebase`. The release has the model, the Lightning training/eval loop and the data loader. It has no data, no viewpoints and no viewpoint-generation script.
3. **Data** (the README just points to the original papers):
   - **FHM:** HF mirror `Jingbiao/RA-HMD`, splits train (8500) / dev_seen (500, used as valid) / test_seen (1000). This matches the paper's Table 5.
   - **HarMeme (Harm-C):** official images and annotations from `github.com/di-dimitrov/mmf` (`data/datasets/memes/defaults`), 3013/177/354, matching Table 5. Labels "somewhat harmful" and "very harmful" were mapped to 1 and "not harmful" to 0. The annotations are identical to the RA-HMD copy.
   - **MAMI:** images from `Jingbiao/RA-HMD` (train 10000, test 1000). Train labels from HF `arch-raven/MAMI` (`misogynous` field); official test labels from `github.com/TIBHannover/multimodal-misogyny-detection-mami-2022` (`data/test.tsv`). Table 5 reports a 100-sample validation set but does not say where it came from. I held out 100 random training memes (seed 0), so I trained on 9900 rather than 10000.
4. **Viewpoints** (`scripts/gen_viewpoints.py`): Qwen2.5-VL-32B-Instruct via vLLM 0.11, using the exact harmful and non-harmful prompts from Appendix B / Fig. 8, with `{title}` set to the meme text. Settings: greedy decoding, max 200 new tokens, max_pixels 1280·28·28. That produced 2 viewpoints for each of 25,544 memes, in about 1 h total.
   - Refusals were rare: 20 harmful-view refusals in total, and none for non-harmful views.
   - The paper does not give its decoding settings.
5. **xlsx files** (`scripts/prep_meta.py`, `scripts/build_xlsx.py`): built `dataset/<DS>/intent_{train,valid,test}.xlsx` in the column layout the loader reads (B = text, C = image, D = label, E = category, F = harmful view, G = non-harmful view), and symlinked images into `dataset/<DS>/Images/intent_<split>/`.
6. **Training/eval:** the unmodified release script, called from `scripts/run_main.sh` with command-line arguments only:
   ```
   python codebase/train_with_lightning_seed.py -output_file runs/<DS>_main/out \
     -bert_path <HF roberta-large> -img_encoding_path <HF Salesforce/blip2-opt-2.7b> \
     -clip_path <HF openai/clip-vit-base-patch16> -data_root codebase/dataset/<DS> \
     -device 0 -epochs 10 -batch_size 48 -seeds 42 43 44 45 46
   ```
   The hyperparameters hard-coded in the release match the paper: lr 1e-5; α=0.5, β=0.01, γ=1e-3, η=0.1 (see below); RoBERTa-large last 2 layers fine-tuned for 4 epochs; BLIP-2 frozen; 4 experts with top-2 routing.

## Discrepancies
1. **code_vs_paper, major** — `models/adaptive_gatting.py: AdaptiveGate.forward`.
   - Paper (Eq. 4–5): soft fusion, h = w·h_v + (1−w)·h_nv, with w = Sigmoid(Linear(Cat(E_v, E_nv))).
   - Code: a 2-way softmax head produces `choose_hate = argmax`, and the enhanced harmful or non-harmful sequence is hard-selected. Gradients from the task loss do not flow through the selection weight; the selector is trained only by L_view.
   - Affects the reported results (the numbers come from this code).
2. **code_vs_paper, minor** — `train_with_lightning_seed.py`: `EarlyStopping(monitor='val_loss', patience=3)`, and the test set is evaluated on the final model.
   - The paper says training runs for 10 epochs and does not mention early stopping.
   - In our runs, 10 of the 15 trainings stopped early, at 5–9 epochs.
   - The code also adds AdamW weight decay 0.01 and a cosine LR schedule, which the paper does not mention.
3. **code_vs_paper, minor** — `models/idmoe.py: Idmoe.forward`. `fusion_output, hyper_loss = expert(...)` followed by `hyper_loss += hyper_loss` overwrites the accumulator. The entailment loss is therefore only that of the last hyperbolic expert (doubled, then divided by 2), not the mean over the hyperbolic experts. The coefficient is 0.1 (`int_beta`) × 0.1 = 0.01 = β, consistent with the paper.
4. **code_vs_paper, minor** — `data/data_management.py: __getitem__`. When an image is smaller than 100 px, has an aspect ratio outside [0.33, 3], or any text is shorter than 4 characters, the sample is silently replaced by a random other sample. This also happens on the test and validation sets, so a few test items are replaced by random (possibly duplicate) items: FHM 3, MAMI 2, HarMeme 1. This is not described in the paper; the effect is ≤0.3%.
5. **code_vs_paper, minor** — `models/adaptive_gatting.py: AdaptiveGate_rand`. It is named and commented as "random selection" (cf. Table 2 "Random Select"), but it always returns the non-harmful viewpoint before enhancement. It is not used in the main experiment.
6. **paper_internal** — Appendix E.11 text vs. Table 13. The text claims "absolute accuracy improvements of 3.96%, 6.50%, and 18.87% over the strongest vanilla VLM baseline". From Table 13 the gains are 75.18−71.32 = 3.86 on FHM and 76.70−75.70 = 1.00 on MAMI. The 6.50 is the gap to "both viewpoints" (70.20), not to the strongest baseline.
