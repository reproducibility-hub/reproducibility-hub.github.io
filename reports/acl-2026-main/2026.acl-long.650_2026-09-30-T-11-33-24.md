## 1. What the paper claims (target: Table 1 and §5.2)
* Identity bias is everywhere in MAD. The IBC = Δ_vanilla − Δ_anonymized is non-zero almost everywhere, and **18 of 20 model–dataset cells have IBC > 0**, i.e. sycophancy dominates.
* Response anonymization collapses Δ (Conformity − Obstinacy, measured at debate round 1) toward 0.
* Secondary (Fig. 3): anonymization lowers Subversion more than Correction. The text example is Qwen-32B on MMLU-ProMed: Subversion −64.3%, Correction −14.9%.

Setup in the paper: 5 homogeneous agents, ring topology with one peer, 1 debate round, temperature 1.0, top-p 0.9, max 2048 new tokens. Data: GPQA-Diamond (198), MMLU Professional Medicine (272), HellaSwag (300 sampled), GSM8K (300 sampled). Models: Qwen2.5-7B/32B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, gpt-oss-20b.

**Interpretation.** The paper's qualitative conclusions reproduce:
* IBC is positive (sycophancy) in the same 18/20 count.
* Vanilla Δ is large for Qwen and Llama.
* Anonymized Δ is near 0 everywhere.
* Mistral and GSM8K show the weakest or negative effects.

Individual values are noisy. With 1 round, 5 agents and often only 40–600 disagreeing (sample, agent) pairs, the sd of IBC across our reruns is 0.01–0.25 (largest for gpt-oss, which has few disagreements because it is either right or truncated). The paper reports single runs without error bars, so many single-cell gaps fall inside or near this noise. Some do not:
* Qwen-7B HellaSwag: IBC 0.15 ± 0.05 vs 0.54.
* Llama-8B GSM8K: 0.47 ± 0.05 vs 0.08.
* Qwen-32B HellaSwag: 0.66 ± 0.14 vs 0.31.

So the ordering of cells by IBC magnitude only partly matches (r = 0.58).

## 4. Why not fully reproduced
* Numbers are outside the tolerance in most cells. This is largely sampling noise (the paper gives no seeds, CIs or std; vLLM sampling is unseeded). A few cells differ systematically, and I found no cause for those. Likely candidates are library-version differences: the published `environment.yml` cannot be the environment that was actually used.
* With no source edits, Mistral is broken under the pinned vLLM 0.13.0. vLLM auto-selects its `mistral_common` tokenizer for this checkpoint, and it tokenizes the HF chat-template string (`<s>[INST] … [/INST]`) as literal text, so the model never sees its control tokens. I verified this by tokenizing the same string with both tokenizers. As released, Mistral shows strong self-bias: MMLU vanilla Δ −0.43 and IBC −0.57 (paper −0.015); GPQA and HellaSwag IBC signs flip. After forcing `tokenizer_mode='hf'`, Mistral is close to the paper, e.g. HellaSwag vanilla Conf/Obst 0.485/0.465 vs paper 0.485/0.449.

## 5. Codebase completeness: MAJOR_FIXES
Changes to released code (uncommitted in `./codebase`; see `git diff`):
1. `model/_engine.py`: for Mistral, set `common['tokenizer_mode'] = 'hf'`. Without it, Mistral prompts lose their chat control tokens under the pinned vLLM. This changes Mistral's results drastically, hence MAJOR_FIXES even though it is one line.
2. `codebase/token`: HF token file added as the README instructs (deleted after the runs so no credential is left behind).

Environment and config issues (MINOR level):
* `environment.yml` cannot be solved (transformers 5.4.0 / hub 1.8.0 vs vllm 0.13.0).
* `scripts/run-*.sh` hard-code `PYTHON=/home/froilanchoi/...`.

Scripts I wrote outside the codebase:

* `table.py`, `make_comparisons.py`, `subcor.py`: build the comparison tables using the released metric functions.

Other observations, left unchanged:
* `debate/evaluator.py::extract_mcq` takes the 2nd character inside the last `{…}`, so junk such as `(T)`, `(1)`, `({)` counts as an answer, and therefore as a disagreement. This matters most for gpt-oss: with max 2048 tokens, 44% of its GPQA responses are truncated, giving no answer, and some parse to junk letters.
* `debate/metrics.py` drops answers that are falsy, so GSM8K answers equal to 0 are dropped.
* Llama prompts get a double BOS (the chat template adds one and the tokenizer adds another).
